Summary: God speaks to Moses from a burning bush and promises to be with him as he leads God's people (Exodus 3-4). Memory line: God is always with me!
Question for ages 2-3: What was burning but did not burn up? (A bush!)
Question for ages 4-8: What did God say to Moses? Why was Moses worried? What did God promise? When do we need to remember God is with us?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for being with me everywhere I go. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Draw a bush with orange and yellow flames, and write "God is with me" around it.
