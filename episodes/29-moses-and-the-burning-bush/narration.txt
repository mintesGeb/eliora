Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Eli was trying to warm his hands by the little porch heater, and Zara was telling him, "Careful, it's hot!" Eli said, "Grandpa Sol, did anything ever burn and not burn up?" Why yes! It's a wonderful story about Moses. Come sit close, my dears.

Long ago, Moses was a shepherd. He took care of his sheep out in the quiet desert. One day, he led his sheep up the side of a big mountain. The sun was warm, and the sheep nibbled the grass.

Then Moses saw something very strange. A little bush was on fire! But the bush did not burn up. The flames danced and glowed, and the leaves stayed green. "How strange!" said Moses. "Why doesn't the bush burn up? I must go and see!"

As Moses came closer, God called out from the bush, "Moses! Moses!" And Moses said, "Here I am!" God said, "Do not come any closer. Take off your sandals, for you are standing on holy ground." Moses took off his sandals, and he covered his face, because he knew that God was there.

God said, "I have seen how my people are suffering in Egypt. I have heard them cry out. I will send you to bring them out." Moses was worried. He said, "Who am I? How can I do this?" God said gently, "I will be with you."

Moses still felt worried. "What if they don't listen to me? I don't speak very well." But God said, "Look at what is in your hand." It was only his shepherd's staff. God showed Moses that with His help, even a simple staff could do wonderful things. And God said, "Your brother Aaron will help you, too."

So Moses said yes. He picked up his staff, said goodbye to the quiet mountain, and he set out to do what God asked, because God had promised, "I will be with you."

So Eli, what do we learn? God is always with us, even when we feel small or worried. We can trust Him! Now let's sing "God Is With Me" together! Here we go!
