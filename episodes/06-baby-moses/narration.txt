Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Today we have a tiny baby visiting next door, and Zara asked, "Grandpa, does God watch over little babies?" So I said, come sit close, and I'll tell you about a baby in a basket.

Long ago, God's people lived in the land of Egypt. A mean king called Pharaoh did not like them. He made a cruel rule that baby boys must be taken away.

One mommy had a beautiful baby boy. She loved him so much! She hid him at home for three months to keep him safe.

When he grew too big to hide, she made a little basket out of reeds. She covered it with sticky tar so no water could get in. Then she gently laid her baby inside.

She put the basket in the tall grass by the river Nile. Float, float, float! The baby's big sister, Miriam, hid nearby and watched to see what would happen.

Soon the king's daughter, the princess, came down to the river to wash. She saw the basket in the tall grass and sent her helper to get it.

She opened the basket, and the baby was crying! The princess felt sorry for him and said, "This must be one of the Hebrew babies."

Then brave Miriam stepped out and asked, "Shall I find a mommy to feed the baby for you?" The princess said, "Yes, go." And Miriam ran and brought the baby's very own mommy!

So the baby's mommy got to take care of him and love him. When he grew older, he went to live with the princess as her son.

The princess named him Moses, because she pulled him out of the water. And one day God would use Moses to lead His people to freedom.

So, friends, does God watch over little babies? Yes! God watched over baby Moses in the basket, and God watches over you, too, wherever you are.

Now let's sing "Floating Basket" together. Rock the baby in your arms with Eli and Pip! Ready? Let's go!
