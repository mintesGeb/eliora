Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were washing Pip's little hooves in a basin of warm water, and Pip was wiggling and giggling. "All clean!" said Eli. Oh, that reminds me of a night when Someone very important washed feet. Come sit close, my dears.

One evening, Jesus sat down for a meal with His twelve friends in a warm upper room. The lamps glowed, and the table was ready. But in those days, people walked on dusty roads in sandals, so their feet were dirty. Usually a servant would wash them. But there was no servant that night.

Nobody picked up the towel. The friends were busy talking about who was the greatest. But Jesus stood up. He took off His robe, and He tied a towel around His waist. Then He poured water into a basin.

Jesus knelt down and began to wash His friends' feet, one by one. Peter was so surprised! "Lord," said Peter, "You will never wash my feet!" But Jesus said gently, "If I do not wash you, you cannot be with Me."

"Then, Lord," said Peter, smiling, "wash not just my feet, but all of me!" Jesus smiled kindly and washed Peter's feet too.

When Jesus finished, He put on His robe and sat down again. "Do you understand what I have done?" He asked. "I am your Teacher and Lord, and I washed your feet. So you should serve each other too. Love one another, just as I have loved you."

My dears, Jesus is the greatest of all, and He served with love. We can serve each other too. Now Pip, are you ready? Let's sing about it! Here we go!
