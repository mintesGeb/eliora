Summary: Jesus washes His friends' feet to show how to serve and love one another (John 13:1-17,34). Memory line: Love one another!
Question for ages 2-3: What did Jesus wash? (His friends' feet!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why was Peter surprised? Why did Jesus serve His friends? How can we serve someone today?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for Jesus, who served with love. Help us love and help others. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Do one kind helping job for someone at home, like setting the table, and say "I love you" as you do it.
