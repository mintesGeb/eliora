Summary: Christmas is about Jesus, the best gift God gave the world (John 3:16, Luke 2). Memory verse: "God so loved the world that He gave His only Son" (John 3:16). Memory line: Jesus is God's best gift!
Question for ages 2-3: Who is God's best gift? (Jesus!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did God give us Jesus? What can we say to God for His gift? How can we share God's love with someone this week?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for sending Jesus, Your best gift. Help us share Your love. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Draw a present with Jesus inside, and write "Thank You, God, for Jesus." Share one act of kindness today.
