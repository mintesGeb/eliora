Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were wrapping presents for their family, and Pip the lamb was sitting on the ribbon. Eli said, "Grandpa Sol, I love giving presents! But what is Christmas really about?" Oh, Eli, that is the best question! Come sit close, my dears.

Presents are fun, and it is nice to give them. But Christmas is about the very best gift of all. A long, long time ago, God gave the whole world a gift. It was not wrapped in shiny paper. It was not in a big box. God's gift was a baby, born in a little stable in Bethlehem.

Do you know who that baby was? It was Jesus! The Bible says, "God so loved the world that He gave His only Son." God loves us so much that He sent Jesus to be with us. Jesus came to show us how much God loves us, and to be our Savior.

An angel told the shepherds, "Good news! A Savior has been born!" The wise men followed a star to find Him. Everyone who found Jesus was filled with joy. And do you know what? Jesus is not just a gift for some people. He is a gift for everyone. For you, and for me, and for every boy and girl in the world.

When we get a present, we say thank you. And we can say thank you to God for His gift, too. We can tell Jesus, "Thank You for coming. I love You!" We can also share God's love with others, like helping a friend, giving a hug, or telling someone about Jesus. That is a gift anyone can give!

Zara asked, "So the best part of Christmas isn't the presents?" Grandpa Sol smiled. "Presents are nice. But the best part is Jesus, the gift God gave us. And we can enjoy everything else, because we know why we celebrate."

So Eli, what do we learn? Christmas is about Jesus! He is God's gift of love to us all. Now let's sing "God's Best Gift" together! Here we go!
