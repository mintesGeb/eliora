Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara's knees went wobble, wobble, wobble before she sang at church. Zara asked, "Grandpa, what if I'm too little to do something big?" Oh, I love that question! So come sit close, and I'll tell you about a man named Gideon!

Long, long ago, God's people stopped obeying God. And guess what came next? An army from Midian came, like a cloud of grasshoppers, with camels too many to count! They took the food. God's people hid in caves. Then they cried out to the Lord, "Please help us!"

Now, Gideon was hiding in a winepress, beating out wheat. Thump, thump, thump! Suddenly, the angel of the Lord sat under an oak tree nearby! And he said, "The Lord is with you, mighty warrior!"

Gideon said, "Me? My family is the weakest, and I am the least in my family!" But the Lord said, "I will be with you." Gideon put meat and bread on a rock, and the angel touched them with his walking stick. Whoosh! Fire jumped out of the rock and burned it all up! Wow!

Gideon wanted to be very sure. So he put a wool fleece on the ground at night. In the morning, the wool was wet, but the ground was dry! He squeezed it. Drip, drip, drip! A whole bowl of water! The next night, the wool was dry, and the ground was wet! God answered both times!

Then thirty-two thousand men came to help. But God said, "Too many! Israel might brag that they saved themselves." So Gideon said, "Anyone who is afraid may go home." And twenty-two thousand went home! Can you believe it?

God said, "Still too many!" So at the water, most men knelt down to drink. But three hundred men lapped water from their hands. Slurp, slurp, slurp! And God said, "With these three hundred, I will save you!"

That night, Gideon sneaked close to the enemy camp. Shh! A man there told his dream. A loaf of barley bread tumbled into camp and knocked a tent flat! His friend gasped, "God will give Gideon the win!" And Gideon bowed down and worshiped God.

Then Gideon gave each man a trumpet, and an empty jar with a torch hidden inside. They tiptoed around the enemy camp in the dark. Gideon blew his trumpet. Toot, toot, toot! Everyone blew their trumpets and smashed their jars. Crash, crash, crash!

The torches shone bright, and they shouted, "A sword for the Lord and for Gideon!" The enemy got all mixed up, and they ran away! God won the battle for them! Hooray!

Do you see? God can do BIG things through little people, because God is with us! When you feel small or scared, you can pray, "God, be with me." And then be brave, and try!

And now, let's sing "Trumpets and Torches" together! Blow your pretend trumpet, toot, toot, with Eli and Pip! Are you ready? Here we go!
