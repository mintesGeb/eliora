Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Today Zara's knees went wobble, wobble before she sang at church. Zara asked, "Grandpa, what if I'm too little to do something big?" So come sit close, and I'll tell you about Gideon.

Long ago, God's people stopped obeying God. An army from Midian came like a cloud of grasshoppers, with camels too many to count! They took the food. God's people hid in caves. Then they cried out to the Lord for help.

Gideon was hiding in a winepress, beating out wheat. Thump, thump! The angel of the Lord sat under an oak tree nearby. He said, "The Lord is with you, mighty warrior!"

Gideon said, "Me? My family is the weakest, and I am the least in my family." The Lord said, "I will be with you." Gideon put meat and bread on a rock, and the angel touched them with his walking stick. Whoosh! Fire came out of the rock and burned it all up!

Gideon wanted to be sure. He put wool on the ground at night. By morning the wool was wet, but the ground was dry. He squeezed it. Drip, drip! A whole bowl of water! The next night the wool was dry, and the ground was wet.

Thirty-two thousand men came. God said, "Too many! Israel might brag that they saved themselves." So Gideon said, "Anyone who is afraid may go home." Twenty-two thousand went home.

God said, "Still too many." At the water, most men knelt down to drink. But three hundred lapped water from their hands. Slurp, slurp! God said, "With these three hundred, I will save you."

That night Gideon sneaked close to the enemy camp. A man there told his dream. A loaf of barley bread tumbled into camp and knocked a tent flat! His friend said, "God will give Gideon the win!" Gideon worshiped God.

Gideon gave each man a trumpet and an empty jar with a torch inside. They tiptoed around the camp. Gideon blew his trumpet. Toot, toot! Everyone blew their trumpets and smashed their jars. Crash!

The torches shone bright, and they shouted, "A sword for the Lord and for Gideon!" The enemy got all mixed up and ran away! God won for them.

God can do big things through little people, because God is with us. When you feel small or scared, pray, "God, be with me," and then be brave and try.
