Summary: God calls Gideon, the least in a weak family, and wins a great battle with just 300 men, trumpets, jars and torches, showing that God's power, not our size, wins (Judges 6-7).
Memory line: When I am small, God is strong!
Question for ages 2-3: What did Gideon's men blow? (Trumpets! Toot, toot!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did God send most of the soldiers home? When do you feel small, and how can God help you be brave?
Prayer: Dear God, when I feel small or scared, thank You that You are with me and You are strong. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Roll up paper into "trumpets" and put a flashlight in a paper cup "jar". Turn off the lights, march around the room, then shout "Toot, toot! When I am small, God is strong!" and switch on your lights.
