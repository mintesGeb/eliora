Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were building a big wall out of wooden blocks, and Pip the lamb was marching all around it. Eli said, "Grandpa Sol, how do you knock down a wall without even touching it?" Ha! That reminds me of a wonderful story. Come sit close, my dears.

Long ago, God's people came to a big city called Jericho. Jericho had tall, thick walls all around it. The gates were shut tight. Nobody could get in. Joshua was the leader, and he looked at those walls and wondered, "How will we ever get inside?"

Then God spoke to Joshua. "I will give you this city. Here is what you must do. March around the city once a day for six days. Seven priests will carry trumpets, and everyone will follow quietly."

So the next morning, everyone lined up. The priests went first with their trumpets. Then came the people, walking quietly, all together. They marched around the city one time, and they went back to camp. The people of Jericho watched from the walls and they did not know what to think.

The next day, the people did it again. March, march, march, around the city, then back home. And the next day, and the next. Six whole days, one time around each day. It was not easy to be so quiet and to wait. But they trusted God.

On the seventh day, they woke up very early. This time, God said, "March around the city seven times!" So they marched, once, twice, three times, four, five, six, seven! Then the priests blew their trumpets, loud and long! And Joshua said, "Shout! For the Lord has given you the city!"

And all the people shouted with a great big shout! And the walls of Jericho shook, and they came tumbling down! God's people walked right in, and God kept Rahab and her family safe, because she had helped God's people.

So Eli, what do we learn? When we trust God and do what He says, even when it's hard to wait, God does great things! Now let's sing "March Around the Wall" together! Here we go!
