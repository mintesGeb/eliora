Summary: Joshua and God's people march around Jericho as God says, and the walls fall down (Joshua 6). Memory line: Trust God and do what He says!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the people blow? (Trumpets!)
Question for ages 4-8: What did God tell Joshua to do? Why was it hard to wait? When do we need to trust God?
Prayer: Dear God, help us trust You and do what You say, even when it is hard to wait. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Build a block wall, march around it seven times with a paper trumpet, then shout and let it tumble.
