Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today, something is missing. Pip! Where is Pip? Eli looked under the bench, behind the flower pot, and even in the laundry basket. "Grandpa, Pip is lost!" Oh dear, Eli, don't worry. We'll find him. And while we look, I'll tell you a story Jesus told about a shepherd and a little lost sheep.

Jesus said, "Once there was a shepherd who had one hundred sheep." One hundred! Baa, baa, baa, baa! Woolly, white, and fluffy, all walking together on the green hills. The shepherd knew every sheep by name, and he loved each one.

One evening, the shepherd counted his sheep. "One, two, three, four, five," and on and on, until he reached ninety-nine. Ninety-nine? Where was number one hundred? One little sheep had wandered away! The shepherd said, "Oh, no!"

Now, what do you think the shepherd did? Did he say, "Oh well, I still have ninety-nine"? No, no, no! He left the ninety-nine safe in the field, and off he went to look for the one!

Up the rocky hill he climbed! Over the dark valley he walked! Through the thorny bushes he pushed. "Little sheep! Where are you?" he called, in a big, loud voice. Then he stopped, and listened. Quiet, quiet, quiet. Not a sound.

Meanwhile, the little sheep was stuck. Her wool was tangled in the thorns, and she was so scared. "Baa," she cried, in a tiny, shaky voice. "Baa... baa..."

And then, the shepherd heard it! "I hear you, little one! I am coming!" He ran, and he found her, and oh, how happy he was! He gently freed her wool, he lifted her up, and he put her on his shoulders. "I found you! I found you!"

All the way home, the shepherd was singing. And when he got home, he called his friends. "Come and celebrate with me! I found my lost sheep!" Hooray! What a party!

Then Jesus smiled, and said, "That is how God feels about each of you. When one of His children is lost, He looks and looks until He finds them. And there is joy in heaven!"

And Eli, look! Do you hear that? Jingle, jingle, jingle! Pip was napping in the garden the whole time! Oh, Eli, you found him! Just like the shepherd!

Friends, you are never too lost, never too small, and never too far away. God always comes looking for you, because He loves you.

And now, let's sing "One Little Lamb" together! Are you ready? Here we go!
