Summary: Jesus tells of a shepherd who leaves ninety-nine sheep to find one lost sheep and celebrates when he finds it, showing how much God loves and searches for each person (Luke 15:3-7).
Memory line: God finds me!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the shepherd do when he found his sheep? (He gave it a big hug! Baa!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did the shepherd go after just one sheep? How does it feel to know that God never stops looking for you?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for loving me so much that You never leave me alone. Thank You for finding me. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Hide a stuffed lamb in the house. One person is the shepherd and searches, calling "I'm coming!" When found, give a big hug and cheer "God finds me!"
