Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Tonight the wind is blowing hard, and the thunder goes boom! Zara held my hand and said, "Grandpa, I'm scared of the storm." So I said, come sit close, and I'll tell you about a big storm and a big God.

One day Jesus taught a huge crowd of people beside a lake. When evening came, Jesus said to His friends, "Let's go over to the other side." So they got into a boat and pushed off from the shore.

Jesus was tired after His long day. He went to the back of the boat, put His head on a soft cushion, and fell fast asleep.

Then a big storm came. Whoosh went the wind! Splash went the waves! The waves crashed over the sides, and the boat began to fill up with water.

Jesus' friends were very scared. Some of them were fishermen, and they had seen many storms, but never one like this! They held on tight as the boat rocked up and down.

They ran to Jesus and woke Him up. "Teacher! Don't You care if we drown?"

Jesus got up. He spoke to the wind, and He said to the waves, "Quiet! Be still!"

And right away, the wind stopped. The waves lay down flat. Everything was completely calm. Shhh.

Jesus looked at His friends and asked them, "Why are you so afraid? Do you still have no faith?"

His friends were amazed. They whispered to each other, "Who is this? Even the wind and the waves obey Him!"

So, friends, when you feel scared, remember that Jesus is with you, and He is stronger than any storm. You can pray, "Jesus, help me be brave," and He will give you His peace.

Now let's sing "Peace, Be Still" together. Rock like a boat, then be still, with Eli and Pip! Ready? Let's go!
