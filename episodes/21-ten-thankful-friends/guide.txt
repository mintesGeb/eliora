Summary: Jesus heals ten men with a skin sickness, but only one comes back to thank Him (Luke 17:11-19).
Memory line: Thank You, Jesus, for every good thing!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the one man say to Jesus? (Thank You!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why do you think the other nine forgot? What are three good things God gave you today? How can we say thank you to Jesus?
Prayer: Dear Jesus, thank You for my family, my food, and my friends. Thank You for loving me. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Name ten things you are thankful for, counting on your fingers. Then say together, "Thank You, Jesus!"
