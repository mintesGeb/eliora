Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Eli got a cookie from Zara, and he gobbled it up and ran off to play. Zara said, "Eli, what do you say?" And Eli turned right around and said, "Thank you, Zara!" Ha! Come sit close, Eli. I'll tell you about ten friends, and only one remembered to say thank you.

Long ago, Jesus was walking toward a village. And there, standing far away at the edge of the road, were ten men. They were very sick. They had a skin sickness, and the law said they had to stay away from everyone. No hugs. No homes. No families. They were so lonely.

But they had heard about Jesus! So all ten called out together, as loud as they could, "Jesus! Master! Please have mercy on us!"

Jesus heard them. He looked at them with kind eyes, and He said, "Go and show yourselves to the priests."

Now, that was a funny thing to say, because they were still sick! But the ten men believed Jesus. So off they went, walking down the road.

And as they walked, something wonderful happened. Their skin became clean and healthy! Every one of them was well! "Look at me!" "Look at my hands!" "I'm healed!" They jumped and laughed, and all ten hurried off to show the priests.

But one man stopped. He turned around and ran back to Jesus, shouting, "Praise God! Praise God!" He fell down at Jesus's feet, and said, "Thank You! Thank You, Jesus!"

Jesus smiled, and said, "Weren't there ten who were healed? Where are the other nine?" Then Jesus said to the thankful man, "Stand up and go. Your faith has made you well."

So Eli, what do we learn? God gives us so many good things. Food, family, and friends! And a thankful heart makes Jesus smile. So let's sing "Thank You, Jesus" together! Sing along, friends! Here we go!
