Summary: Jesus tells about a Samaritan who stops to help a hurt stranger, showing that our neighbor is anyone who needs help (Luke 10:25-37).
Memory line: Be kind to everyone like Jesus!
Question for ages 2-3: Who helped the man on the road?
Question for ages 4-8: Why do you think the Samaritan stopped when the others walked by? Who could you help this week?
Prayer: Dear Jesus, thank You for loving everyone. Help me be kind to my neighbors. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Make a "kindness kit" together: a bandage, a snack and a drawing, and give it to a neighbor or friend.
