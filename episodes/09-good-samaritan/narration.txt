Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Today a little boy next door fell off his bike, and Eli ran to help him up. Zara asked, "Grandpa, who is my neighbor?" So I said, come sit close, and I'll tell you what Jesus said.

One day a teacher of the law asked Jesus a big question. Jesus said, "Love the Lord your God with all your heart. And love your neighbor as you love yourself." The man asked, "And who is my neighbor?" So Jesus told a story.

A man was walking down a long, dusty road from Jerusalem to Jericho. Suddenly, robbers jumped out! They took his things, hurt him, and ran away. The poor man lay beside the road, and he could not get up.

Soon a priest came walking down the road. He saw the hurt man. But he did not stop. He crossed to the other side and kept on walking.

Then a Levite came along, a man who worked at God's temple. He saw the hurt man too. But he did not stop either. He crossed to the other side and kept on walking.

Next came a man from Samaria, called a Samaritan. Many people did not like Samaritans, and Samaritans were not friends with the man on the road. But when the Samaritan saw him, his heart felt very sad. He felt pity.

The Samaritan ran to him! He poured oil and wine on the hurt places to help them heal. Then he wrapped them up with soft bandages.

He lifted the man onto his own donkey. Clip, clop, clip, clop! They went slowly to an inn, and the Samaritan took care of him all night long.

In the morning, the Samaritan gave two silver coins to the innkeeper. "Please take care of this man," he said. "If it costs more, I will pay you back when I come again."

Then Jesus asked, "Which of the three was a neighbor to the man the robbers hurt?" The teacher said, "The one who helped him." And Jesus said, "Go and do the same."

So a neighbor is anyone who needs our help, and we can be kind to everyone, even when it is not easy. You can share, help, and say kind words, just like the Samaritan.

Now let's sing Be a Good Neighbor together. Give a big helping hug to someone next to you, with Eli and Pip! Ready? Let's go!
