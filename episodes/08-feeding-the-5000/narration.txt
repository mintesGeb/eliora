Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Today Eli shared his crackers with Pip, and Zara asked, "Grandpa, does a little bit really help?" So I said, come sit close, and I'll tell you about a boy with a very small lunch.

One day a huge crowd followed Jesus up a grassy hill by a lake. They wanted to see Him heal sick people and hear Him teach. There were five thousand men, plus women and children too!

The day went on and on, and everyone started to get hungry. Jesus asked His friend Philip, "Where can we buy bread for all these people to eat?"

Philip said, "Even if we worked for many months, we could not buy enough bread for everyone to have just a bite!"

Then Andrew came with a boy. "Here is a boy with five small loaves of barley bread and two little fish. But how far will that go among so many?"

The boy gave his lunch to Jesus. Jesus said, "Have the people sit down." So everyone sat down on the soft green grass.

Jesus took the five loaves and gave thanks to God. Then He broke the bread and the fish, and His friends passed them out. Pass, pass, pass! And there was more, and more, and more!

Everyone ate as much as they wanted. Big people, little people, everyone was full! Yum!

Then Jesus said, "Gather the pieces that are left over. Don't waste anything." His friends picked up twelve baskets full of leftovers!

The people were amazed at what Jesus had done with one boy's little lunch.

So, friends, when you give what you have to Jesus, even something small, He can use it to do something big. You can share your toys, your snack, and your smile.

Now let's sing "Five Little Loaves" together. Count to five on your fingers with Eli and Pip! Ready? Let's go!
