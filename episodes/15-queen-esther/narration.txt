Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara has a little problem. She has to stand up in front of her whole class tomorrow, and her knees are wobbling like jelly! Zara asked, "Grandpa, how can I be brave when I am scared?" Oh, that is a wonderful question, Zara. Come sit close, and I'll tell you about a girl named Esther, who became a queen, and who was braver than she ever thought she could be.

Long, long ago, Esther was a young Jewish girl, and she lived with her cousin Mordecai. He was kind and wise, and he loved her very much. One day, the king of Persia chose Esther to be his queen! Imagine that! A beautiful palace, shiny crowns, and servants everywhere. But Mordecai said softly, "Esther, do not tell anyone you are a Jew." So Esther kept her secret.

Now, there was a man named Haman. Oh, he was proud and grumpy! "Bow down to me!" he growled. But Mordecai would only bow to God. Haman was so angry! He made a terrible plan. He tricked the king into making a law, that all God's people would be in danger!

Mordecai heard the news, and he was so worried. He sent a message to Esther. "Esther, you must go to the king, and ask him to help your people. Maybe God made you queen for exactly this time."

Esther gulped. Gulp! Her knees wobbled, and she whispered, "But Mordecai, no one may go to the king without being invited! It is very, very dangerous!" She was scared. Really scared! But then she took a deep breath, and she said, "Gather everyone to pray for me. I will go. And if I must, I must."

Step, step, step. Esther walked down the long, shiny hall. Her heart went thump, thump, thump! She stood at the door of the throne room, and the whole room went quiet. The king looked up. And then, he smiled, and he held out his golden scepter! "What is it, Queen Esther? Ask me anything!"

Esther was so wise. She invited the king and Haman to a special dinner. And at the dinner, she said, in a clear, brave voice, "Oh, king, someone has made a plan to hurt me, and my people." "Who would dare?" cried the king. And Esther pointed. "That wicked man, Haman!" Gasp! Haman's face turned white as a sheet!

The king changed the law, and God's people were safe. Hooray! Everyone cheered, and sang, and danced! And Mordecai was honored, because God took care of His people, just as He promised.

Now, friends, was Esther scared? Yes! But she was brave anyway, because God was with her. Being brave does not mean you are never afraid. It means you do the right thing, even when your knees wobble!

So, Zara, tomorrow when your knees wobble, you can whisper, "God is with me!" And friends, God is with you, too!

And now, let's sing "Brave Queen Esther" together! Stand up tall, friends! Are you ready? Here we go!
