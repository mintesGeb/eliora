Summary: Esther, a Jewish girl who became queen of Persia, bravely goes to the king to save her people from Haman's plan, trusting that God put her there for such a time as this (Esther 2-8).
Memory line: God is with me, I can be brave!
Question for ages 2-3: What did Esther wear on her head? (A crown! Big and shiny!)
Question for ages 4-8: Esther was scared but did the right thing anyway. When is it hard for you to be brave, and how can remembering that God is with you help?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for being with me when I am scared. Help me to be brave and do the right thing. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Put on a paper crown, march down the hallway like Esther, and say "God is with me!" at the end. Take turns, and cheer for each other.
