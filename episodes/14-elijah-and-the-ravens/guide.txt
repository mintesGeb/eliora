Summary: God tells Elijah to hide by the brook Cherith, where ravens bring him bread and meat every morning and evening, showing that God takes care of His children (1 Kings 17:1-6).
Memory line: God takes care of me!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the ravens bring Elijah? (Bread! Caw, caw!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why do you think God used ravens? When do you worry about having what you need, and how can you remember that God takes care of you?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for taking care of me every day, with food, with family and with love. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Flap your arms like ravens and "deliver" bread (a roll or a paper bread) to each family member while saying "God takes care of me!" Then thank God for your next meal together.
