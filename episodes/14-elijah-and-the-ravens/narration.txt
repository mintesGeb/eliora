Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Eli's tummy went grumble, grumble, grumble. Eli asked, "Grandpa, what if there is no food for dinner?" Oh, that is a worrying thought, Eli. So come sit close, and I'll tell you about a prophet named Elijah, and some very surprising helpers!

Long, long ago, there was a wicked king named Ahab. He did not obey God. He did not listen to God. And God's people forgot to say thank you to the One who gave them everything.

So God sent Elijah with a message. Elijah walked right up to the king, and he said, in a big, brave voice, "As the Lord lives, there will be no rain, and no dew, until I say so!" Oh my! The king did not like that one bit!

Then God spoke softly to Elijah. "Go east, and hide by the brook Cherith. You may drink from the brook. And I have told the ravens to bring you food!" Ravens? Black, noisy, flappy ravens? Elijah wondered, but he said, "Yes, Lord!" And off he went.

Elijah came to the little brook. Splash, splash, splash! The water was cool and clear. He cupped his hands, and he took a big, long drink. Ahhh! Just as God had promised!

And then, in the morning, flap, flap, flap! Caw! Caw! Caw! Here came the ravens, swooping down from the sky! Each one had bread in its beak! Plop! Plop! Plop! Fresh bread on the ground! Can you believe it?

Elijah laughed out loud! "Thank You, Lord! Bread for breakfast, delivered by birds!" And that was not all. In the evening, flap, flap, flap, caw, caw, caw, here they came again, with meat! Yum, yum, yum!

Day after day, morning and evening, the ravens came. Not one day did they forget! Not even a crumb was late! Elijah never went hungry, because God took care of him.

But the days went by, and no rain fell. The grass turned brown. The ground turned dry. And one day, Elijah looked at the brook, and it was getting smaller. Smaller and smaller. Little Elijah might have felt worried. But he remembered, God has taken care of me every single day!

Now, friends, think about it. Ravens are not usually kind to share. But God can use anyone, even noisy black birds, to take care of the people He loves!

And God is taking care of you, too. When your tummy goes grumble, He knows. When you feel worried, He sees you. He takes care of the birds, and He takes care of you!

And now, let's sing "Ravens Bring Bread" together! Flap your wings, friends! Are you ready? Here we go!
