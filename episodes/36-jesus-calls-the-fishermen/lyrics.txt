[Verse 1]
Fishermen by the water blue
Fishing all night, nothing to do
Empty nets, the morning came
Then Jesus called each one by name

[Chorus]
Follow Me, follow Me
Come and walk along with Me
Jesus calls, and we say yes
Follow Me, He loves us best!
