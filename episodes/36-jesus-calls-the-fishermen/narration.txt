Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were sitting on the edge of the porch with a little toy net, pretending to fish, and Pip the lamb watched the net like a cat. "Grandpa Sol, we caught nothing all morning!" said Eli. Ah, my dear, that is just how this story begins. Come sit close, and I will tell you.

Long ago, by a big blue lake called the Sea of Galilee, there lived some fishermen. Simon, who was also called Peter, and his brother Andrew, and two more friends named James and John. They worked hard every day, catching fish in their boats and nets.

One night, they fished and fished. They threw their nets again and again. But when the sun came up, their nets were empty. Not even one little fish! The fishermen were tired, and they were washing their nets on the shore.

Then Jesus came walking along the beach, and a large crowd followed Him, because they wanted to hear about God. Jesus saw Simon's boat, and He stepped in. "Please push out a little from the shore," He said. Simon did, and Jesus sat down in the boat and taught the people.

When Jesus finished teaching, He said to Simon, "Now, take the boat out into deeper water, and let down your nets for a catch." Simon was tired. He said, "Master, we worked all night and caught nothing. But because You say so, I will let down the nets."

And do you know what happened? As soon as the nets went down, they filled with fish! So many fish that the nets began to tear! They called to their friends in the other boat to come and help, and both boats filled up with fish, until they almost sank!

Simon Peter fell on his knees. "Go away from me, Lord," he said, "I am a sinful man." But Jesus was not angry. He said kindly, "Do not be afraid. From now on, you will catch people." Jesus wanted them to follow Him and tell others about God's love.

So the fishermen pulled their boats up on the shore. They left everything, their boats, and their nets, and they followed Jesus! Peter, Andrew, James and John became Jesus' very first helpers.

So Zara, so Eli, what do we learn? When Jesus says, "Follow Me," we can say yes and go with Him, because He is the best friend and the kindest leader of all. Now let's sing "Follow Me" together! Here we go!
