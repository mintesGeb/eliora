Summary: Jesus fills the fishermen's empty nets with fish and calls Peter, Andrew, James and John to follow Him (Luke 5:1-11, Matthew 4:18-22). Memory line: Jesus says, Follow Me!
Question for ages 2-3: What filled the nets? (Fish!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did Simon listen to Jesus even though he was tired? What did the fishermen do next? How can we follow Jesus this week?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for calling us to follow Jesus. Help us listen and say yes. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Cut paper fish, write a way to follow Jesus on each one (be kind, share, pray), and put them in a box "net".
