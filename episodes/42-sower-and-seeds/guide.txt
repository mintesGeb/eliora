Summary: Jesus tells the story of a farmer scattering seeds on different soils, and God's Word grows best in a listening heart (Luke 8:4-15). Memory line: God's Word grows in me!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the farmer plant? (Seeds!)
Question for ages 4-8: Which seeds did not grow? Which seeds grew into a big harvest? How can our hearts be like good soil?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for Your Word. Help our hearts be good soil so it can grow in us. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Plant a seed in a small cup of soil, water it each day, and talk about how God's Word grows in us.
