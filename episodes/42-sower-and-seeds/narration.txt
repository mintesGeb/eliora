Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were planting seeds in little clay pots, and Pip was sniffing the soil. "Grandpa Sol, will they really grow?" asked Eli. Oh, that reminds me of a story Jesus told about seeds. Come sit close, my dears.

One day, Jesus sat in a little boat by the lake, and a big crowd sat on the grassy bank to listen. Jesus told them a story. "A farmer went out to plant seeds," He said. The farmer walked across the field, scattering seeds everywhere.

Some seeds fell on the hard path. Swoop! The birds came and ate them up. Some seeds fell on rocky ground. They popped up fast, but the roots were shallow, and when the sun got hot, the little plants dried up.

Some seeds fell among thorns. The thorns grew tall and crowded the little plants, so they could not grow. But some seeds fell on rich, good soil. Those seeds grew tall and strong, and they made a wonderful harvest!

Later, Jesus told His friends what the story meant. The seed is God's Word. When we hear God's Word and keep it in our hearts, like good soil, it grows and grows, and our lives are full of good things.

So, my dears, let's listen to God's Word with open hearts. Pip, are you listening? Good! And now we are going to sing about it. Ready? Here we go!
