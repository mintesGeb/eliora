Summary: Wise men follow a star to find young Jesus and give Him gifts (Matthew 2:1-12). Memory line: Jesus is the best gift of all!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the wise men follow? (A star!)
Question for ages 4-8: How did the wise men travel? What gifts did they bring? What gift can we give Jesus?
Prayer: Dear Jesus, we love You. Thank You for being the best gift of all. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Make a paper star, hide it in the house, and follow clues to find it. Then say, "Jesus is the best gift!"
