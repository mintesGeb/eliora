Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were looking out the window at the sky, and Eli said, "Grandpa Sol, I see a star! Let's follow it!" And Pip the lamb trotted right behind him! Ha! Come sit close, my dears. I'll tell you about some men who followed a very special star.

Long ago, far away in the East, lived some wise men. They loved to study the stars. One night, they saw a new star in the sky. It was bright, and it was different from all the others. "This star means a great king has been born!" they said. So they packed their bags, and they got on their camels, and they began to follow the star!

They traveled for a long, long time. Over hills, across deserts, and through quiet valleys. At night, they looked up and saw the star shining ahead of them. Shine, shine, shine!

At last, they came to the city of Jerusalem, and they asked, "Where is the child who has been born king? We saw His star and have come to worship Him!" King Herod heard about it, and he was worried. But the wise men did not stop. They kept looking up, following the star.

And then, the star stopped right over a little house in Bethlehem! The wise men jumped off their camels and went inside. And there was Mary, and there was young Jesus! The wise men were so happy! They bowed down, and they gave Jesus gifts they had carried all the way: gold, frankincense, and myrrh. Each gift was very special, fit for a king!

That night, God told the wise men in a dream, "Do not go back to Herod. Go home a different way." And so they did! They climbed back on their camels, and they traveled home by another road, with happy hearts, because they had seen the King!

So Eli, what do we learn? Jesus is the best gift of all, and the wise men gave Him their very best gifts! We can give Jesus our love, our songs, and our thank-yous. And now let's sing "Follow the Star" together! Here we go!
