Summary: Jesus is baptized by John in the Jordan River, the Spirit comes down like a dove, and God the Father says He loves His Son (Matthew 3:13-17). Memory line: God loves Jesus, and God loves us!
Question for ages 2-3: What came down from the sky? (A dove!)
Question for ages 4-8: What did John say to the people? What did God say about Jesus? How do we know God loves us?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for loving Jesus and for loving us. Help us trust in Him. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Cut a dove shape from white paper and write "God loves me" on it. Hang it where you can see it.
