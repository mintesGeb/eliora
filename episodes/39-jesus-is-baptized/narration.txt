Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were splashing their hands in a big bowl of water, and Pip the lamb was drinking from it. "Look, Grandpa Sol, a little pretend river!" said Zara. Oh, that reminds me of a very special day at a real river. Come sit close, my dears.

Long ago, a man named John lived near the Jordan River. He wore rough clothes, and he told everyone, "Get ready! Someone wonderful is coming!" Many people came to hear him. They said sorry for the wrong things they had done, and John baptized them in the river.

One day, a man walked down to the water. It was Jesus! John looked at Him and knew Him right away. "Oh, Jesus," said John, "I should be baptized by You!"

But Jesus said, "Let it be this way now. It is right to do what God asks." So John did what Jesus asked, and he gently lowered Jesus into the cool, clear water.

And when Jesus came up out of the water, something amazing happened. The sky opened up, and a soft white dove came down and rested on Jesus. It was the Spirit of God!

Then a voice came from heaven, a loving, happy voice. It was God the Father! He said, "This is My Son, whom I love. I am very pleased with Him."

Can you imagine how happy that made everyone? God loves Jesus so much. And, my dears, because we trust in Jesus, God loves us too.

Now Pip, did you hear that? We are God's loved children! And we are going to sing about it. Ready? Here we go!
