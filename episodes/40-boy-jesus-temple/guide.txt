Summary: Twelve-year-old Jesus is found in the temple listening to the teachers and asking wise questions (Luke 2:41-52). Memory line: Jesus loves God's house!
Question for ages 2-3: Where did Mary and Joseph find Jesus? (In the temple!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why were Mary and Joseph worried? What was Jesus doing in the temple? How can we love God's house?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for Your house and Your Word. Help us listen and learn about You. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Play hide-and-seek with a stuffed toy, then draw the building where we learn about God.
