Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were playing hide-and-seek, and Eli hid so well that Zara could not find him! "Where is Eli?" she said, looking all around. Oh, that reminds me of a time long ago when a boy could not be found. Come sit close, my dears.

When Jesus was twelve years old, His family traveled to the big city of Jerusalem for a special festival. Mary and Joseph walked with Jesus, along with many friends and family. The golden city was full of happy people.

When the festival was over, everyone started the long walk home. Mary and Joseph thought, "Jesus must be with our friends." They walked a whole day. But when they looked for Jesus, He was not there!

"Have you seen Jesus?" Mary asked. Everyone shook their heads. Mary and Joseph were worried, so they hurried back to Jerusalem. They looked and looked, in every street and every doorway.

After three days, they climbed the steps of the temple. And there, sitting with the teachers, was Jesus! He was listening and asking wise questions, and everyone who heard Him was amazed.

Mary hugged Jesus tight. "Son," she said, "we looked everywhere for you!" Jesus said gently, "Why were you looking for Me? Didn't you know I would be in My Father's house?"

Then Jesus went home with Mary and Joseph, and He obeyed them. He kept on growing, and God and everyone around Him loved Him.

Now Pip, did you hear that? Jesus loved to be in God's house. And we are going to sing about it. Ready? Here we go!
