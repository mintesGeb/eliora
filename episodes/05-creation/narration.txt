Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Tonight the sky is full of stars. Eli looked up and asked, "Grandpa, who made the stars?" So I said, come sit close, and I'll tell you how God made everything.

In the very beginning, there was nothing at all. No sky, no trees, no animals, no people. Everything was dark and empty. But God was there.

On day one, God said, "Let there be light!" And there was light! God called the light day, and the dark night.

On day two, God made the big blue sky way up high, with the water below it.

On day three, God gathered the water into seas and made dry land appear. Then He made grass and flowers and trees, apple trees, orange trees, and tall green palms!

On day four, God made the bright sun for the day, the silver moon for the night, and all the twinkly stars. Twinkle, twinkle!

On day five, God filled the seas with fish, splash, splash, and the sky with birds, flap, flap! Big whales and tiny fish, eagles and little sparrows.

On day six, God made the animals on the land. Lions and elephants, puppies and kittens, bunnies that hop and lambs that say baa! Then God made the first man and woman, Adam and Eve. God made them to be like Him, and God loved them very much.

God looked at everything He had made, and He said, "It is very good!"

On day seven, God rested from all His work, and He made that day special and holy.

So, friends, who made the stars? God did! God made the whole world, and God made you, too. You are special, and God loves you very much.

Now let's sing "Day by Day God Made It" together. Count the days on your fingers with Eli and Pip! Ready? Let's go!
