Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara was looking everywhere for her little yellow hair ribbon. Under the cushion, behind the chair, even in Pip's wool! "Found it!" she cried at last, and she danced around the porch. Oh, that reminds me of a story Jesus told about a lost coin. Come sit close, my dears.

Once, there was a woman who had ten shiny silver coins. They were very special to her, and she kept them safe. She counted them one by one. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine... and then she stopped. Where was the tenth coin? One coin was missing!

The woman did not say, "Oh well, I still have nine." No, no! She lit a little lamp, because her house was dark, and she picked up her broom. She looked under the table. She looked behind the jars. She swept the floor, sweep, sweep, sweep, and she searched carefully, until she found it.

And then, there it was! A little shiny coin twinkling in the lamplight. "I found it!" she cried. She was so happy! She ran to call her friends and neighbors. "Come, rejoice with me! I found my lost coin!" And everyone celebrated together.

Jesus smiled and said, "In the same way, there is joy in heaven over every single person that God finds." My dears, you are so precious to God. If you ever feel lost or small, remember that God looks for you and loves you, and He is so happy when you are found. Now Pip, are you ready? Let's sing about it! Here we go!
