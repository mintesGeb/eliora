Summary: Jesus tells about a woman who lost one coin and searched until she found it, and how heaven rejoices over everyone God finds (Luke 15:8-10). Memory line: God finds me and loves me!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the woman lose? (A coin!)
Question for ages 4-8: How did the woman look for her coin? How did she feel when she found it? How does God feel about each one of us?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for loving me and finding me. Help me know I am precious to You. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Hide a coin in the room, search together with a flashlight, and cheer loudly when you find it.
