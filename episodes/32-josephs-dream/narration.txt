Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were setting up the little nativity figures on the table, and Pip the lamb sat down right next to the donkey. Eli picked up the figure of Joseph and said, "Grandpa Sol, what was Joseph like?" Ah, Joseph was a very good man. Come sit close, my dears.

Long ago, Joseph lived in Nazareth. He was a carpenter, and he worked hard making things out of wood. Joseph loved God, and he was going to marry a young woman named Mary.

But one day, Joseph heard that Mary was going to have a baby. Joseph was a good man, and he was very worried. He wanted to do the right thing. He lay awake at night, thinking and thinking.

Then one night, as Joseph slept, God sent an angel in a dream. "Joseph," said the angel, "do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife. The baby inside her is from God. She will have a son, and you will name Him Jesus, because He will save His people."

The angel also said, "This will happen just as God promised long ago. The Lord's Son will be called Immanuel, which means, 'God with us.'" And when Joseph woke up, his heart was full of peace. He was not worried anymore.

Joseph did exactly what the angel told him. He took Mary to be his wife, and he took good care of her. He worked hard to make a safe home for her. And when baby Jesus was born, Joseph named Him Jesus, just like God said.

Joseph listened to God, and he obeyed right away, even when it was not easy. He loved and cared for Mary and for Jesus every day.

So Eli, what do we learn? God is with us! Jesus is Immanuel, God with us. And when God tells us what is right, we can listen and obey, just like Joseph. Now let's sing "God With Us" together! Here we go!
