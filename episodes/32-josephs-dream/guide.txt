Summary: An angel tells Joseph in a dream not to be afraid, and Joseph obeys God and takes care of Mary and baby Jesus (Matthew 1:18-25). Memory line: Jesus is Immanuel, God with us!
Question for ages 2-3: What was Joseph's job? (A carpenter!)
Question for ages 4-8: What did the angel say to Joseph? What does Immanuel mean? How did Joseph show he trusted God?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for being with us. Help us listen to You and obey with a happy heart. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Make something from craft sticks like Joseph the carpenter, and say "God is with us" each time you add a piece.
