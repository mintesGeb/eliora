Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Look, it's raining today! Zara was a little sad. She said, "Grandpa, Dad promised we could go to the park, but now it's raining. Does God keep His promises?" Well, come sit close, and I'll tell you a true story from the Bible about the very first rainbow.

A long, long time ago, the people on the earth stopped loving God. They were mean, and they hurt each other. That made God very sad. But there was one man who loved God and walked with Him every day. His name was Noah.

One day God told Noah, "A great flood is coming. I want you to build a big boat called an ark. Make it out of wood, and make it huge! Give it three floors, a door on the side, and a window at the top." Noah had never seen a flood like that. But Noah did everything just as God told him.

So Noah and his three sons got to work. Saw, saw! Hammer, hammer! Bang, bang, bang! They worked for a long, long time. Some people probably laughed at Noah. A giant boat, so far from the sea? But Noah trusted God.

When the ark was finished, the animals came. They came two by two, a male and a female of every kind! Big elephants, tall giraffes, little rabbits, birds that flap and snakes that wiggle. Stomp, stomp, hop, hop, flap, flap! And Noah brought food for every one of them.

Then Noah went inside with his wife, his three sons, and their wives. Eight people in all. And then God Himself shut the door.

Pitter patter. Then the rain began. It rained for forty days and forty nights! The water rose higher and higher, until it covered even the tall mountains. But inside the ark, Noah's family and all the animals were safe and dry, because God was taking care of them.

After many, many days, God sent a wind, and the water began to go down. The ark came to rest on the mountains of Ararat. Noah opened the window and sent out a dove. But it came right back, because there was water everywhere. A week later, Noah sent the dove again. This time it came back with a fresh olive leaf in its beak! That meant the land was drying. The next time, the dove did not come back at all. It had found a home.

Then God said, "Come out of the ark!" So the door opened, and out came Noah's family and all the animals, two by two, into the sunshine. And the very first thing Noah did was build an altar and thank God.

Then God put a beautiful rainbow in the clouds. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple! And God made a promise: "Never again will a flood destroy the whole earth. When you see the rainbow, remember My promise."

So, friends, does God keep His promises? Yes! God always keeps His promises. People sometimes forget, but God never does. So next time you see a rainbow, you can say, "Thank You, God, for keeping Your promises!"

Now, let's sing "Two by Two" together. Stand up, and march like the animals with Eli and Pip! Ready? Let's go!
