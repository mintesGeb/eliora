Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were hanging a shiny star on the Christmas tree, and Pip the lamb was nibbling on a ribbon. Eli said, "Grandpa Sol, who was the very first person to hear that Jesus was born?" That is a wonderful question! Come sit close, my dears. It was some shepherds, out on a quiet hill.

Long ago, near the little town of Bethlehem, some shepherds were out in the fields at night. They were taking care of their sheep. The night was cool and dark, and the stars twinkled up above. The sheep were sleeping, and the shepherds sat by a small fire, keeping watch.

All at once, the sky filled up with a bright, beautiful light! An angel stood right in front of them! The shepherds were very frightened. But the angel said, "Do not be afraid! I have good news for you, and for everyone. Today, in the town of David, a Savior has been born! He is Christ the Lord!"

Then the angel told them how to find Him. "You will find a baby wrapped in soft cloths, lying in a manger." And suddenly, the sky was full of angels, singing! "Glory to God in the highest, and peace on earth!" It was the most beautiful music the shepherds had ever heard.

When the angels went back to heaven, the shepherds looked at each other. "Let's go to Bethlehem right now!" they said. "Let's see what God has told us!" And they hurried off, leaving the sheep safe on the hill, with their staffs in their hands and their hearts full of joy.

They came to a little stable. And there they found Mary, and Joseph, and the baby, lying in a manger, just as the angel had said! The shepherds were so happy. They knelt down, and they smiled at baby Jesus.

Then the shepherds went out and told everyone what they had seen and heard. They went back to their sheep, thanking God and singing for joy! 

So Eli, what do we learn? Good news is for sharing! The shepherds told everyone about Jesus, and we can tell others about Him too. Now let's sing "Go Tell It Gladly" together! Here we go!
