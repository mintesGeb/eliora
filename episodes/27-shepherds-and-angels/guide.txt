Summary: Angels tell shepherds that Jesus the Savior is born, and the shepherds hurry to see Him and tell everyone (Luke 2:8-20). Memory line: Good news is for sharing!
Question for ages 2-3: Who sang in the sky? (The angels!)
Question for ages 4-8: What did the angel tell the shepherds? Where did they find baby Jesus? What good news can we share this Christmas?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for sending Jesus. Help us share the good news with everyone. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Make a paper star, and tell one person the good news that Jesus was born.
