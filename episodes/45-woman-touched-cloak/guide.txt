Summary: A woman who had been sick for twelve years touches the edge of Jesus' cloak and is healed, and Jesus says her faith made her well (Mark 5:25-34). Memory line: Jesus loves to help me!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the woman touch? (Jesus' cloak!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did the woman want to touch Jesus' cloak? What did Jesus say to her? When can we ask Jesus for help?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You that Jesus hears me and helps me. I trust You. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Hold hands in a circle and take turns saying one thing you want to ask Jesus to help with, then pray together.
