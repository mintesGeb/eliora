Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Eli had a tummy ache, and Zara gave him a big, gentle hug and sat beside him until he felt better. Pip even rested his little head on Eli's lap. Oh, that reminds me of a woman who needed help very much. Come sit close, my dears.

Once, there was a woman who had been sick for twelve long years. She had tried and tried to get better, but nothing worked. She felt tired and sad and a little bit lonely.

One day, she heard that Jesus was coming to her town. "If I can just touch His cloak," she thought, "I will be well." She was too shy to ask out loud, because there was a huge crowd. People pushed and bumped and hurried everywhere.

But she did not give up. Step by step, she slipped through the crowd, closer and closer. Then she stretched out her hand and touched the edge of Jesus' cloak. Just one little touch! And right away, she felt strong and well again.

Jesus stopped. "Who touched My clothes?" He asked. His friends said, "Lord, there are so many people!" But Jesus kept looking around kindly. Then the woman came forward, trembling, and she told Him everything.

Jesus smiled so gently at her. "Daughter," He said, "your faith has made you well. Go in peace." My dears, Jesus is never too busy for us. He hears us when we pray, and He loves to help. Now Pip, are you ready? Let's sing about it! Here we go!
