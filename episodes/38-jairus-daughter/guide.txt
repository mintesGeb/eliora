Summary: Jesus heals the daughter of Jairus and tells him, "Do not be afraid, only believe" (Mark 5:21-24, 35-43). Memory line: Do not be afraid, only believe!
Question for ages 2-3: What did Jesus say to the little girl? (Get up!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why was Jairus afraid? What did Jesus tell him? What can we do when we feel scared?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You that Jesus is always with us. When we are scared, help us believe. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Draw a picture of something that scares you, then draw Jesus holding your hand beside it.
