Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were tucking Pip the lamb into a little bed made of a basket and a blanket. "Shh, Pip is sleeping," whispered Eli. "Wake up, Pip!" giggled Zara, and Pip popped up with a wiggle. That reminds me of a father, and a little girl, and a very special morning. Come sit close, my dears.

Long ago, there was a man named Jairus. He was an important leader in his town, but today he felt very small and very scared. His little girl, only twelve years old, was very sick. He loved her so much.

Jairus knew just who could help. He hurried through the streets until he found Jesus beside the sea, with a big crowd all around. Jairus knelt down at Jesus' feet. "Please come," he said. "My little girl is very sick. Please come and touch her, and she will live."

Jesus did not say, "I'm too busy." Jesus said yes, and He went with Jairus. The whole crowd pressed close, walking along with them.

But before they reached the house, some people came running. "Jairus," they said sadly, "your daughter has died. There is no need to trouble Jesus anymore."

Jairus' heart nearly broke. But Jesus heard, and He turned to Jairus with kind eyes. "Do not be afraid," said Jesus. "Only believe."

When they came to the house, people were crying and making a lot of noise. Jesus said, "Why all this crying? The child is not dead, she is only sleeping." Some people laughed, because they did not understand. Jesus knew exactly what He was going to do.

Jesus gently sent everyone outside. He took only the girl's mother and father, and three of His friends, and He went into her room. There she lay, quiet and still.

Jesus took her little hand in His. In a gentle, loving voice He said, "Little girl, get up!" And right away, her eyes opened! She sat up, and she stood up, and she walked across the room!

Oh, how her mother and father cried happy tears! Jesus smiled and said, "Please give her something to eat." Jesus cared about everything she needed. Nothing is too big for Jesus, and nothing is too small.

And that, my dears, is why we can say, "Do not be afraid, only believe!" Now Pip, did you hear that? Pip is wide awake, and we are going to sing about it. Ready? Here we go!
