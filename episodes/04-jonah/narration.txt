Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Today Eli did something wrong, and he ran and hid under the table. He asked me, "Grandpa, can I hide from God?" So I said, come sit close, and I'll tell you about a man who tried to run away from God.

Long ago, God spoke to a man named Jonah. God said, "Go to the big city of Nineveh. Tell the people to stop doing wrong things." But Jonah did not want to go. So he ran the other way!

Jonah went down to the sea and got on a ship going far, far away. He went down inside the ship, lay down, and fell fast asleep.

Then God sent a great wind. Whoosh! The waves splashed high, and the ship rocked back and forth. The sailors were so scared! They threw things into the sea to make the ship lighter.

Jonah told the sailors, "This storm is because of me. I ran away from God. Throw me into the sea, and the sea will be calm." So they did. Splash! And right away, the sea became still and quiet.

But God was still taking care of Jonah. God sent a big fish, and gulp! The fish swallowed Jonah. Jonah was inside the big fish for three days and three nights.

Inside the fish, Jonah prayed to God. He said, "I'm sorry, God. Thank You for saving me." Then God spoke to the fish, and the fish spit Jonah out onto dry land. Bleh!

God spoke to Jonah again: "Go to Nineveh." And this time, Jonah obeyed. He walked through the big city and told the people God's message.

The people of Nineveh listened! Everyone, even the king, said sorry to God and stopped doing wrong things.

And God forgave them all, because God is kind and full of love. He gives second chances.

So, friends, we can never hide from God, because He is everywhere and He loves us. When we do something wrong, we can say, "I'm sorry, God," and He forgives us.

Now let's sing "Jonah, Jonah, Turn Around" together. Swim like a big fish with Eli and Pip! Ready? Let's go!
