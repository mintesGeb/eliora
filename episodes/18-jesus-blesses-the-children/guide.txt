Summary: Parents bring their children to Jesus. The disciples try to send them away, but Jesus welcomes and blesses them, saying the kingdom of God belongs to those who are like children (Mark 10:13-16).
Memory line: Jesus has a lap for me!
Question for ages 2-3: What did Jesus give the little children? (Big hugs!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why do you think the disciples sent the children away? How do you think the children felt when Jesus called them? What does it mean to trust like a child?
Prayer: Dear Jesus, thank You for loving children. Thank You that I am never too small for You. I love You! Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Take turns sitting on a grown-up's lap, and let them whisper a blessing: "God loves you, and I do too!" Then give a big family group hug.
