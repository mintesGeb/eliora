Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli had a big plan. They wanted to go to the town fair, but Grandpa Sol said, "Not today, it's too busy." Eli folded his arms and said, "Grown-ups always say we're too small." Oh, I know that feeling, Eli. Come sit close, and I'll tell you about a day when Jesus showed everyone just how special children are.

Long ago, Jesus was teaching the people. Many, many people had come, and they were listening to every word. And guess who else wanted to see Jesus? Mothers and fathers, with their little children! Some were babies, some were toddlers, and some were just about your size!

"Please, Jesus," they said, "will You bless our children?" But the disciples frowned. "Shh! Not now! Jesus is busy. Children, go away!" And the little ones looked down, and their smiles went away.

But Jesus heard. And He was not happy! Jesus said, in a strong, clear voice, "Stop! Let the little children come to Me! Do not stop them!"

The children looked up, and their eyes lit up! Whoosh! They ran to Jesus, giggling, and skipping! Toddlers waddled. Babies were carried. Everyone wanted to be near Him!

Jesus smiled the biggest, kindest smile. He picked up the little ones, one by one, and He gave them great big hugs! He put His hands on their heads, and He blessed them, softly and sweetly. "God loves you," He whispered.

Then Jesus looked at the grown-ups, and said, "The kingdom of God belongs to people who are like these children. They trust, and they love, and they believe, just like a child does."

Can you imagine? The most important thing Jesus did that day was to stop and hug the little children! Children are never too small, never too noisy, and never too busy for Jesus!

So Eli, are you too small? Not at all! Jesus has a lap, and it has room for you. And friends, Jesus has a lap for you, too!

And now, let's sing "Let the Children Come" together! Open your arms wide, friends! Are you ready? Here we go!
