Summary: At a wedding in Cana, Jesus turns water into wine, His first miracle (John 2:1-11). Memory line: Do whatever Jesus says!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the servants fill the jars with? (Water!)
Question for ages 4-8: What did Mary tell the servants? Why did the servants know the miracle was real? How can we do what Jesus says today?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for Jesus. Help us trust Him and do what He says. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Fill clear cups with water, add a drop of red juice, and remember how Jesus changed the water at the wedding.
