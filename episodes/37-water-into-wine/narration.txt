Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were pouring water from a big jug into little cups for a tea party, and Pip the lamb sat in the middle, wearing a tiny party hat. "Grandpa Sol, our juice ran out!" said Zara. Oh my, that reminds me of a wedding long ago. Come sit close, my dears.

Long ago, in a little town called Cana, there was a wedding party. Everyone was dressed in their best clothes, and there was music, and dancing, and good food. Jesus was invited, and so was His mother Mary, and His disciples.

But in the middle of the party, something went wrong. The wine ran out! In those days, running out of drink at a wedding was a big problem. The family hosting the party would be very embarrassed.

Mary noticed, and she went to Jesus. She said, "They have no more wine." Then Mary turned to the servants and said, "Do whatever He tells you." Mary trusted Jesus.

Nearby stood six big stone jars, the kind people used for washing. Jesus said to the servants, "Fill the jars with water." So the servants hurried to fill them, one bucket at a time, right up to the very top.

Then Jesus said, "Now take some out and bring it to the man in charge of the feast." The servants did exactly as Jesus said. And when the man tasted it, his eyes grew wide! The water had become wine, and it was the best wine of the whole party!

The man in charge was surprised. He said to the bridegroom, "Most people serve the best wine first. But you have saved the best until now!" The servants knew where it came from, because they had filled the jars themselves.

This was the very first miracle Jesus did. The disciples saw it, and they believed in Him. Jesus showed that He has the power to do wonderful things, and He cares about the little things in our lives, too.

So Zara, so Eli, what do we learn? When we trust Jesus and do what He says, even small things become wonderful! Now let's sing "Do Whatever He Says" together! Here we go!
