Summary: Jesus rose from the dead on the third day, and Mary Magdalene and the disciples saw Him alive (Matthew 28, Luke 24, John 20:1-18). Memory line: Jesus is alive!
Question for ages 2-3: Was the tomb empty or full? (Empty!)
Question for ages 4-8: Who was the first person to see Jesus alive? How did she feel? Why is Easter such a happy day?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You that Jesus is alive. Thank You for loving us so much. We love You, Jesus. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Draw an empty tomb with a rolled-away stone and a bright sunrise, and write "He is alive!" on it.
