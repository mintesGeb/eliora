Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were holding a little basket of colored eggs, and Pip the lamb wore a tiny yellow ribbon. "Grandpa Sol, why is Easter so happy?" asked Zara. Ah, my dear, it is the happiest story of all. Come sit close, and I will tell you.

Long ago, Jesus died on a cross. His friends were very sad. They gently wrapped His body in clean cloth, and they laid Him in a stone tomb, a quiet cave cut into a hillside. Then a big, heavy stone was rolled in front of the door.

For two days, the friends cried and stayed close together. They did not know what would happen next. But Jesus had told them, "I will rise again on the third day."

Early on the third morning, while it was still dark, Mary Magdalene and some other women walked to the tomb. They carried sweet-smelling spices. On the way they wondered, "Who will roll away the big stone for us?"

But when they came to the tomb, the stone was already rolled away! The tomb was open, and it was empty! Then two angels in shining clothes said, "Why are you looking for the living among the dead? He is not here. He is risen, just as He said!"

Mary stood outside the tomb, crying. Then she heard someone say, "Mary." She turned around, and it was Jesus! He was alive! Mary was so full of joy that she ran to tell the others, "I have seen the Lord!"

That same evening, Jesus came and stood with His friends, and He said, "Peace be with you." They were so happy! Jesus was truly alive, and He showed them that He had won over sin and death forever.

So Zara, so Eli, what do we learn? Jesus is alive! He loves us so much that He gave Himself for us, and now He lives forever. Easter is about Jesus. Now let's sing "He Is Alive" together! Here we go!
