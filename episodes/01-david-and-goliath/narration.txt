Hello, friends! Welcome to Grandpa Sol's porch. Zara, Eli, and little Pip are here too. Today Eli asked me a big question. He said, "Grandpa, what do I do when something is way too big for me?" Well, come sit close. I want to tell you a true story from the Bible, about a boy who faced something much, much bigger than him.

Long ago, the army of Israel was camped on one hill. Their enemies, the Philistines, were camped on another hill. And in the valley between them stood a giant named Goliath. He was over nine feet tall! He wore a bronze helmet and heavy armor, and he carried a huge spear.

Every morning and every evening, Goliath shouted, "Send someone to fight me!" And every day, the soldiers of Israel were afraid. Nobody went.

One day, a young shepherd boy named David came to the camp. He was bringing bread and cheese to his big brothers. When David heard Goliath shouting, he asked, "Who is this man who makes fun of the armies of the living God?"

David went to King Saul and said, "I will go and fight him." The king said, "You can't. You are only a boy!" But David answered, "When I took care of my sheep, God saved me from a lion and a bear. He will save me from this giant too."

So King Saul gave David his own armor and his own helmet. But they were so big and so heavy, David could hardly walk! Wobble, wobble! So David took them off.

Instead, David took his shepherd's staff. He went down to the stream, and he chose five smooth stones. He put them in his bag. And he took his sling in his hand.

When Goliath saw David, he laughed. "Ha! You are just a boy!" But David stood calm and brave. He said, "You come against me with a sword and a spear. But I come against you in the name of the Lord, the God of Israel. The battle belongs to the Lord!"

David ran toward the giant. He put a stone in his sling, and he swung it round and round. Whoosh! The stone flew through the air, and it hit Goliath right on the forehead. And down he fell. Thud!

The soldiers of Israel cheered! God had given David the victory. And everyone saw that the God of Israel is real, and He is mighty.

So, friends, was David brave because he was big? No! David was brave because he trusted God, and God is bigger than any giant. When something feels big and scary to you, you can tell God about it. Because God is big, and you can be brave!

Now, let's sing David's song together. Stand up, and do the actions with Eli and Pip. Ready? Let's go!
