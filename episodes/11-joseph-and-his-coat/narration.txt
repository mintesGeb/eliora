Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Today Eli's friend got a new kite, and Eli felt grumpy inside. Zara asked, "Grandpa, did anyone in the Bible ever feel jealous?" So I said, come sit close, and I'll tell you about Joseph.

Long ago, a man named Jacob lived in Canaan. He had twelve sons. Joseph was seventeen, and he helped his big brothers take care of the sheep. Baa, baa!

Jacob loved Joseph more than his other sons. So he made Joseph a beautiful coat. But when the brothers saw it, they were jealous. They could not say one kind word to him.

One night Joseph had a dream. He told his brothers, "We were tying up bundles of grain in the field. My bundle stood up tall. And your bundles bowed down to mine!" The brothers did not like that!

Then Joseph had another dream. "The sun, the moon, and eleven stars bowed down to me!" he said. His father asked, "What kind of dream is that?" But Jacob kept thinking about it.

One day the brothers were far away with the sheep. Jacob said, "Joseph, please go and see if your brothers are okay." So Joseph walked and walked, tap, tap, tap, until he found them at a place called Dothan.

The brothers saw him coming from far away. "Here comes that dreamer!" they said. They made a very mean plan. But Reuben, the oldest brother, said, "Don't hurt him! Just put him in this empty well." Reuben wanted to save Joseph later.

When Joseph came, the brothers pulled off his beautiful coat. They put him into the well. Thump! It had no water in it.

Soon some traders came by with their camels. They were carrying spices to Egypt. Judah said, "Let's not hurt our brother. Let's sell him." So they pulled Joseph out and sold him for twenty pieces of silver. Clink, clink! The traders took Joseph away to Egypt.

Then the brothers put goat's blood on Joseph's coat and took it to their father. Jacob thought a wild animal had hurt Joseph, and he cried and cried. But Joseph was not alone. Later the Bible tells us, "The Lord was with Joseph." God had a big plan for him!

God is with us always, even on sad and hard days. When you feel jealous, be happy for your friend, say a kind word, and remember God is with you too.
