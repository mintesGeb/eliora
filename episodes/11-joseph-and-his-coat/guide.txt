Summary: Joseph's jealous brothers take his beautiful coat and sell him to traders going to Egypt, but God is with Joseph and has a plan for him (Genesis 37; 39:2).
Memory line: God is with me always!
Question for ages 2-3: What special present did Joseph's father give him?
Question for ages 4-8: Why were Joseph's brothers jealous? What can you do when you feel jealous of someone?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for being with me every day. Help me be happy for others and kind when I feel jealous. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Color a paper "Joseph's coat" with many colors, and for each color name one thing you are thankful for or one friend you can be happy for.
