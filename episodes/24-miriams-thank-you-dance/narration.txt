Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were getting ready for Thanksgiving dinner. Eli had a wooden spoon and a pot, and he was banging it like a drum! Bang, bang, bang! "Eli, what are you doing?" said Zara. "I'm saying thank you to God with a big noise!" said Eli. Come sit close, Eli. I'll tell you about a lady who said thank you to God with a tambourine and a dance.

Long ago, God's people, the Israelites, had been slaves in Egypt. But God had set them free! They walked out of Egypt, and Moses led them, and they felt so happy. But then, they came to a big, wide sea. Behind them, they heard the sound of the king's army chasing them. Rumble, rumble, rumble! The people were scared. "We are stuck!" they said. "The sea is in front of us, and the army is behind us!"

But Moses said, "Do not be afraid. God will take care of us." And God told Moses to hold his staff out over the sea. And Moses did. And then, all night long, a strong wind blew. And do you know what happened? The water split in two! There was a wide, dry path right through the middle of the sea, with walls of water on each side!

"Hurry! Go!" said Moses. And all God's people walked through the sea on dry ground. Mothers carried babies. Children held their grandpas' hands. Even the sheep and the goats walked through! Not one person was left behind. And when they were all safe on the other side, God closed up the water again, and the army could not get to them.

The people looked back at the big sea. They were so safe! They were so free! They were so thankful! Moses and the people sang a song to God. And Moses's sister, whose name was Miriam, picked up her tambourine. Shake, shake, shake! And all the women followed her, with tambourines of their own, and they danced! Miriam sang, "Sing to the Lord, for He is greatly honored!"

They twirled, they clapped, they jumped, they sang! They said thank you to God with their whole hearts. And God was so pleased to hear them!

So Eli, what do we learn? When God helps us, we can say thank you in big, happy ways! We can sing, and clap, and dance! And now, let's sing "Shout for Joy" together! Grab your tambourine, friends! Here we go!
