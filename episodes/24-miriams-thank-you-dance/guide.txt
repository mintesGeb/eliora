Summary: God opens the sea so His people can cross safely, and Miriam leads everyone in a thank-you dance (Exodus 14-15). Memory verse: "Shout for joy to the Lord, all the earth" (Psalm 100:1).
Memory line: Thank You, God, I shout for joy!
Question for ages 2-3: What did Miriam play? (A tambourine!)
Question for ages 4-8: How did God take care of His people at the sea? How did they say thank you? How can we say thank you to God today?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for taking care of us. Thank You for our food and our family. We shout for joy! Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Make a tambourine from a paper plate and dried beans, then dance and say three things you are thankful for.
