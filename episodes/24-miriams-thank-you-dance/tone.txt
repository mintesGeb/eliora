A warm, expressive grandfather storyteller reading a picture book to small children. Friendly, bright, clear voice in a comfortable medium-high pitch, never deep or gruff. Natural, unhurried storytelling pace, like talking, not singing: no sing-song and no musical rhythm. Lively but natural character voices: a playful small voice for Eli, a cheerful voice for Zara, a calm, brave voice for Moses, a worried, frightened murmur for the crowd, and a bright, joyful voice for Miriam. Keep the same loudness and pitch from the first word to the last. Not shouting, not like a preacher, no exaggerated excitement, and no background music.
