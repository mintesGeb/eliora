A warm, expressive grandfather storyteller reading a picture book to small children. Friendly, bright, clear voice in a comfortable medium-high pitch, never deep or gruff. Natural, unhurried storytelling pace, like talking, not singing: no sing-song and no musical rhythm. Lively but natural character voices: a playful small voice for Eli, a cheerful voice for Zara, a gentle, soft, wondering voice for Mary, a warm, kind and bright voice for the angel Gabriel, and a warm, delighted voice for Elizabeth. Keep the same loudness and pitch from the first word to the last. Not shouting, not like a preacher, no exaggerated excitement, and no background music.
