Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were lighting the first candle on the Advent wreath. Eli said, "Grandpa Sol, why do we light a candle and wait for Christmas?" Zara said, "Because something wonderful is coming!" That's right! Come sit close, my dears. I'll tell you how the waiting for Jesus began.

Long ago, in a small town called Nazareth, lived a young woman named Mary. Mary loved God very much. She sang little songs while she swept the floor, and she was kind to everyone she met.

One day, Mary was home when the room filled up with a soft, shining light! And there stood an angel named Gabriel! Mary was very surprised. She held her hands to her heart. But the angel smiled kindly and said, "Do not be afraid, Mary! God is very happy with you!"

Then the angel said, "You are going to have a baby boy, and you will name Him Jesus. He will be called the Son of God! He will be a king forever, and His kingdom will never end!"

Mary was puzzled. She said, "How can this be?" And the angel told her, "Nothing is impossible with God!" The angel also told her a happy secret: her cousin Elizabeth, who was very old, was going to have a baby too! Mary smiled. God could do anything!

Mary thought about it quietly. She wasn't scared anymore. She folded her hands and said, "I am the Lord's servant. Let it be just as you said." And she said yes to God, with a happy heart! Then the angel went back to heaven, and the room was quiet again.

Soon, Mary went to visit her cousin Elizabeth. And when Elizabeth saw her, she cheered! Her baby jumped for joy inside her! Elizabeth said, "God has blessed you, Mary!" And Mary sang a happy song of thanks to God. "My soul is full of joy in God my Savior!" they sang together.

So Eli, what do we learn? God keeps His promises! He sent His Son, Jesus, just as He said. And when God asks us to trust Him, we can say yes with a happy heart! Now let's sing "Yes, Mary Said Yes" together! Here we go!
