Summary: The angel Gabriel tells Mary she will have a baby named Jesus, and Mary says yes to God (Luke 1:26-45). Memory verse: "Nothing is impossible with God" (Luke 1:37).
Memory line: Nothing is too hard for God!
Question for ages 2-3: Who came to visit Mary? (An angel!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did the angel say do not be afraid? What did Mary say to God? When has God asked you to trust Him?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for sending Jesus. Help us trust You like Mary did. We say yes to You! Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Light a candle together (with a grown-up) or place a paper star in the window and say together, "Nothing is impossible with God!"
