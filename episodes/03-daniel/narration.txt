Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Today Zara looked a little worried. She said, "Grandpa, some kids laughed when I prayed before lunch. Should I stop praying?" So I said, come sit close, and I'll tell you about a man who kept praying, even when it was hard.

Long ago, a man named Daniel lived in a big kingdom far from his home. Daniel loved God and talked to Him every day. He was wise and honest, and King Darius liked him very much.

The king wanted to put Daniel in charge of the whole kingdom. But some leaders were jealous. They looked and looked for something wrong that Daniel did, but they found nothing at all. So they said, "The only way to catch Daniel is through his God."

They went to the king and said, "King Darius, make a new law. For thirty days, nobody may pray to anyone except you. Anyone who does will be thrown into the lions' den." The king signed the law, and it could not be changed.

Daniel heard about the new law. So what did he do? He went home, opened his window, knelt down, and prayed to God three times a day, just like he always did.

The jealous leaders saw him praying, and they ran to tell the king. King Darius was very sad, because he loved Daniel. He tried all day to save him, but the law could not be changed.

So Daniel was put into the den with the hungry lions, and a big stone was rolled over the opening. The king said, "Daniel, may your God save you!" That night the king could not eat, and he could not sleep.

Early in the morning, the king hurried to the den and called out, "Daniel! Did your God save you?" And Daniel answered, "Yes, King! My God sent His angel, and he shut the lions' mouths. They did not hurt me!"

The king was so happy! Daniel was lifted out, and he did not have a single scratch, because he trusted in God.

Then King Darius told everyone in his kingdom, "Daniel's God is the living God. He saves and He rescues!"

So, friends, Daniel kept talking to God, even when it was hard, and God took care of him. You can talk to God anytime, in the morning, at lunch, and at bedtime. He always listens.

Now let's sing "Daniel Prayed" together. Fold your hands and roar like a lion with Eli and Pip! Ready? Let's go!
