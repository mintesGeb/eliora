Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were lighting the second candle on the Advent wreath, and Pip the lamb was watching the flame flicker. Eli said, "Grandpa Sol, who got ready for Jesus before Jesus was born?" Good thinking, Eli! Come sit close, my dears. I'll tell you about a baby named John.

Long ago, there lived a kind old priest named Zechariah, and his wife, Elizabeth. They loved God very much. But they were sad, because they had no children, and they were getting old.

One day, Zechariah was serving in God's temple. Suddenly, an angel stood beside the altar! Zechariah was very frightened. But the angel said, "Do not be afraid, Zechariah! God has heard your prayer. Your wife Elizabeth will have a baby boy, and you will name him John."

The angel said, "John will bring you joy and gladness. He will help many people get ready for the Lord." Zechariah could hardly believe it. "How can this be? We are so old!" he said. The angel said, "I am Gabriel. I stand in God's presence, and I was sent to tell you this good news. But because you did not believe, you will not be able to speak until it happens."

When Zechariah came out, he could not say a word! The people were puzzled, and he used his hands to show them what had happened. Then he went home. And soon, Elizabeth was going to have a baby, just as God had promised!

Elizabeth was so happy. "God has been so kind to me!" she said. And when baby John was born, everyone wanted to name him after his father. But Elizabeth said, "No, his name is John." Zechariah took a little tablet and wrote, "His name is John." Right away, he could speak again! And the first thing he did was praise God!

So Eli, what do we learn? God keeps His promises, and His timing is always just right! John grew up to help people get ready for Jesus. Now let's sing "God's Perfect Time" together! Here we go!
