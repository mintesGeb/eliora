Summary: An angel tells Zechariah that he and Elizabeth will have a son named John, who will get people ready for Jesus (Luke 1:5-25, 57-66). Memory line: God keeps His promises in His perfect time!
Question for ages 2-3: What was the baby's name? (John!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why were Zechariah and Elizabeth sad? What did the angel promise? What did John grow up to do?
Prayer: Dear God, help us trust You while we wait. Thank You for keeping Your promises. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Light a candle (with a grown-up) and talk about something you are waiting for. Pray and thank God that He is never late.
