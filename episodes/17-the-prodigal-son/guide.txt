Summary: Jesus tells of a son who leaves home, wastes everything, and returns sorry. His father runs to welcome him with a feast, showing how God forgives and welcomes us home (Luke 15:11-32).
Memory line: God's love welcomes me home!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the father do when he saw his son? (He ran and gave a big hug!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did the son feel afraid to go home? How do you think the father felt? What can we do when we make a mistake?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for forgiving me when I say sorry. Thank You for always welcoming me with open arms. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Take turns walking toward each other slowly while saying "I'm sorry." The other person runs up and gives a big hug and says "Welcome home!"
