Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Eli is sad. He said some mean words to Zara, and he knocked down her tower of blocks. "Grandpa, I was so naughty. I don't think anyone will want to play with me now." Oh, Eli. Come sit close. Let me tell you a story Jesus told, about a father and his son who made a big mistake.

Once there was a father who had two sons. The younger son came to him and said, "Father, give me my share of the money. I want to go away!" The father felt sad, but he gave it to him. And off the younger son went, to a faraway land, with a bag full of coins, clink, clink, clink!

He had a wonderful time! He bought fancy clothes, and big dinners, and he threw parties for all his friends. "Look at me! I'm rich!" he laughed. But the money went down, and down, and down. Until, one day, it was all gone. Poof! Not one coin left.

And then there was no food in the land. His tummy rumbled, grumble, grumble. His friends were gone. All alone, he took a job feeding pigs. Squeal, squeal, squeal! He was so hungry that even the pig food looked yummy. He sat in the mud, and he said sadly, "At my father's house, even the servants have plenty to eat. I will go home, and say, I am sorry."

So he began to walk. Slowly, slowly, his feet were tired, and his clothes were dirty. He practiced what he would say. "Father, I made a mistake. I am not good enough to be your son."

But look! Far down the road, who was that? It was the father! Every day, he had been watching the road, and waiting. And when he saw his son, he ran! Run, run, run! He threw his arms around him, and kissed him! "My son! My son is home!"

"Father," the son sobbed, "I am so sorry." But the father did not even listen to the sorry part. "Bring the best robe! Put a ring on his finger! Shoes for his feet! Let's have a feast, because my son was lost, and now he is found!" Hooray!

Jesus told this story to show us how God loves us. Even when we make mistakes, even when we say sorry with shaky voices, God runs to us with open arms.

Eli, did you hear that? When we do something wrong, and we say, "I'm sorry," God does not push us away. He welcomes us home.

And now, let's sing "Welcome Home" together! Open your arms wide, friends! Are you ready? Here we go!
