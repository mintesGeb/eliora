Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Eli had two little cookies, and he looked at them for a long time. Then he gave one to Zara! "Here, you have one too," said Eli. Zara smiled so big. Oh, that reminds me of a story about giving. Come sit close, my dears.

One day, Jesus sat near the big temple in Jerusalem, watching people put their gifts into a big treasure box. Clink! Clank! Some rich people came in fancy robes, and they dropped in lots and lots of shiny coins. It made a very loud noise. Everyone looked at them.

Then a poor widow came, quietly and softly. She wore a plain old shawl. She did not have much at all. She reached into her little pouch, and she dropped in two tiny coins. Clink, clink. They made hardly any sound at all.

Jesus called His friends close. "Look," He said, "this poor widow has put in more than all the others." His friends were surprised. How could two tiny coins be more than all those big gifts?

Jesus explained. "The rich gave a little from what they had left over. But she gave everything she had, with all her heart." My dears, Jesus sees every gift, big or small, and He loves a happy giving heart most of all. Now Pip, are you ready? Let's sing about it! Here we go!
