Summary: Jesus watches people give gifts at the temple and says a poor widow who gave two small coins gave the most, because she gave from her heart (Mark 12:41-44). Memory line: I give with a happy heart!
Question for ages 2-3: How many coins did the widow give? (Two!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did Jesus say the widow gave the most? What can we give to God and to others? How does giving make us feel?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for everything You give us. Help us give with happy, loving hearts. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Put a few coins in a jar for someone in need, and think of one kind thing you can give away, like a hug, a drawing or a toy.
