Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli built a big boat out of couch cushions. "We're sailors!" said Eli. Then the wind began to blow, and the porch door went bang! Eli jumped. "Grandpa, it's a storm!" Don't worry, Eli. Come sit close. I'll tell you about a man named Paul, who sailed through the biggest storm of all.

Long ago, a man named Paul loved Jesus and told everyone about Him. Some people did not like that, and Paul was put on a big wooden ship, to sail far across the sea to a city called Rome. Soldiers and sailors were on board, and Paul's friend Luke was with him too.

At first, the sea was calm. But then, whoosh! A mighty wind came roaring! The waves grew tall, the sky grew dark, and the ship tossed up and down, up and down. The sailors worked hard to hold on. Day after day, the storm did not stop.

No sun. No stars. The sailors were tired, and so very scared. They thought, "We will never get home." Everyone on that ship was afraid, everyone but one.

That night, Paul was praying. And God sent an angel, who said, "Do not be afraid, Paul! God will keep you safe, and everyone on the ship too!" Paul stood up, tall and calm. "Friends!" he said, "Take heart! I believe God. Not one of us will be lost!"

Fourteen nights passed. Then one dark night, the sailors heard waves crashing on the rocks. Land! Paul said, "Let's eat some bread, so we are strong." He thanked God, and he broke the bread, and everyone felt braver.

In the morning, they saw a beach. The sailors tried to steer the ship to shore, but crunch! The ship got stuck, and the waves began to break it apart. "Swim!" shouted the captain. "Grab a plank, and float!"

One by one, every person jumped into the water. Some swam. Some held on to pieces of the ship. And splash, splash, splash, they all reached the sandy shore. Every single one was safe, just like God promised!

Can you imagine? The storm was big, but God was bigger! Eli, were you scared of the wind? That's okay. When we are scared, we can remember God is with us in every storm.

So friends, whenever you hear a big wind, say, "God is with me!" And now, let's sing "Safe in the Storm" together! Hold on tight, friends! Here we go!
