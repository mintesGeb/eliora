A warm, expressive grandfather storyteller reading a picture book to small children. Friendly, bright, clear voice in a comfortable medium-high pitch, never deep or gruff. Natural, unhurried storytelling pace, like talking, not singing: no sing-song and no musical rhythm. Lively but natural character voices: a worried small voice for Eli, a calm strong voice for Paul and a gentle kind voice for the angel, and a loud urgent voice for the captain's 'Swim!'. Keep the same loudness and pitch from the first word to the last. Not shouting, not like a preacher, no exaggerated excitement, and no background music.
