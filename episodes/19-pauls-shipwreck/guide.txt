Summary: Paul is on a ship caught in a huge storm. An angel tells him not to be afraid, and God keeps everyone on the ship safe, just as He promised (Acts 27).
Memory line: God is with me in every storm!
Question for ages 2-3: Who was with Paul in the storm? (God!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why were the sailors scared, and why was Paul calm? What can we say when we feel afraid? Has God ever helped you feel brave?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for being with me when I'm scared. Thank You for keeping me safe. I trust You. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Build a cushion boat together. Rock side to side like a storm, then say, "God is with me!" and sit still and calm.
