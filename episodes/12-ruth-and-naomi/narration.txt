Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Today Zara's best friend moved to a new house far away, and Zara felt sad. She asked, "Grandpa, how do you stay close to someone you love?" So I said, come sit close, and I'll tell you about Ruth and Naomi.

Long ago, a woman named Naomi lived far from her home. Her husband had died, and so had her two sons. Naomi was very sad. But she was not alone. Her son's wife, Ruth, was right there beside her.

Naomi decided to go home to Bethlehem. She told Ruth, "Go back to your own family. They will take care of you." Ruth could have said goodbye. But Ruth held on tight.

Ruth said, "Where you go, I will go. Where you stay, I will stay. Your people will be my people, and your God will be my God." What a loyal friend!

So Ruth and Naomi walked, and walked, and walked all the way to Bethlehem. Their feet were tired, but they walked side by side. When they got there, the town was happy to see Naomi.

They had very little food. So Ruth said, "Let me go to the fields. I will pick up the grain that is left behind." Ruth worked hard in the hot sun. Pick, pick, pick!

The field belonged to a kind man named Boaz. He saw Ruth working and said, "Stay in my field. Drink from our water jars. I will make sure you are safe." Ruth was so thankful!

Boaz told his workers, "Leave some extra grain on the ground for her." That night Ruth came home with so much food. Naomi smiled for the first time in a long time. "God is taking care of us!" she said.

Boaz saw that Ruth was kind and brave. In time, Boaz and Ruth got married. Naomi was so happy. God had given her a new family.

Soon, Ruth and Boaz had a baby boy named Obed. Naomi held him close and hugged him. She was not sad anymore. Her heart was full of joy.

Obed's grandson was David, and David became a great king. God used Ruth's kind and faithful heart to bless many people.

God wants us to be loyal friends too. When someone is sad, stay close, help them, and be kind. And remember, God never leaves us.
