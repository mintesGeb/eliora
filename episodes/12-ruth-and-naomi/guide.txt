Summary: Ruth stays loyal to her grieving mother-in-law Naomi, goes with her to Bethlehem, works in Boaz's field, and God blesses them with a new family (Ruth 1-4).
Memory line: God never leaves me!
Question for ages 2-3: Who stayed close to Naomi?
Question for ages 4-8: How did Ruth show she was a loyal friend? How can you stay close to someone who is sad?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for never leaving me. Help me be a kind and loyal friend. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Make a "Where you go" friendship card: trace two hands side by side, write a kind message inside, and give it to a friend or family member.
