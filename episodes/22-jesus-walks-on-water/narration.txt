Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Last night the thunder went BOOM, and Zara hid under her blanket. This morning she asked, "Grandpa, what can I do when I feel scared?" Come sit close, Zara. I'll tell you about the night Jesus walked on the water.

One day Jesus fed a big crowd with five loaves and two fish. Then He told His disciples, "Get into the boat and go to the other side of the lake." So they did. Jesus sent the crowd home. Then He went up on a mountain all by Himself to pray.

Night came. The boat was far out on the lake. Whoosh! The wind blew hard against them. Splash! The waves pushed the little boat up and down, up and down.

Very early in the morning, while it was still dark, Jesus went out to them. And guess what? He was walking on top of the water! Step, step, step, right across the waves!

The disciples saw Him coming. They were so scared! "It's a ghost!" they cried out. They did not know it was Jesus.

Right away Jesus called to them. "Be brave! It is I. Don't be afraid."

Peter said, "Lord, if it's You, tell me to come to You on the water." Jesus said, "Come." So Peter climbed out of the boat. His feet touched the water, and he walked on it! Peter was walking to Jesus!

But then Peter saw the strong wind. Whoosh! He got scared, and he began to sink. Glub, glub, glub! "Lord! Save me!" he cried.

Right away Jesus reached out His hand and caught him. "Your faith is so small," Jesus said. "Why did you doubt Me?"

Then Jesus and Peter climbed into the boat, and the wind stopped. Shhh. Everything was calm. The disciples in the boat worshiped Jesus. They said, "You really are the Son of God!"

When we feel scared, we can keep our eyes on Jesus, because He is strong and He holds us. So when you feel afraid, you can pray, "Jesus, help me!" and remember His strong hand.

Now let's sing Keep My Eyes on Jesus together. Hold out your hand, just like Jesus did, with Eli and Pip! Ready? Let's go!
