Summary: Jesus walks on the stormy lake, calls Peter to come to Him, and catches Peter's hand when he begins to sink (Matthew 14:22-33).
Memory line: I keep my eyes on Jesus!
Question for ages 2-3: What did Jesus walk on? (The water!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did Peter start to sink? What can you do when you feel scared?
Prayer: Dear Jesus, when I am scared, help me look to You and hold Your hand. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Play "Eyes on Jesus": one person walks a line on the floor (a rope or tape "wave") while looking only at a picture of Jesus held at the end. Take turns, then hold hands and say the memory line together.
