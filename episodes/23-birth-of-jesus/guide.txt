Summary: Jesus is born in Bethlehem, and angels tell the shepherds the good news (Luke 2:1-20).
Memory line: Jesus is born, God sent His love!
Question for ages 2-3: Where was baby Jesus's bed? (A manger)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did the angels tell the shepherds first? How did the shepherds feel when they saw Jesus? What is the best gift God gave us?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for sending Jesus to us. Thank You for loving us so much. Happy birthday, Jesus! Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Make a paper star together and put it on a window. Then sing "Glory to God!" three times.
