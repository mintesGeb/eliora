Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were wrapping a present, and Pip the lamb kept sitting right on the ribbon! "Grandpa Sol," said Zara, "why do we give presents at Christmas?" Come sit close, my dears. I'll tell you about the very first Christmas, and the best gift ever given.

Long ago, in a town called Nazareth, lived a young woman named Mary, and her husband-to-be, Joseph. God had sent an angel to tell Mary a wonderful secret. She would have a baby boy, and His name would be Jesus. He would be God's own Son, sent to love the whole world!

Then one day the king said, "Everyone must go to the town of their family, to be counted!" So Joseph and Mary had to travel all the way to Bethlehem. It was a long, long walk, over hills and along dusty roads. Mary rode on a little donkey, and Joseph walked right beside her.

At last they came to Bethlehem. The streets were full of people! Joseph knocked on one door. "No room," said the innkeeper. He knocked on another door. "No room." And another. "Sorry, no room here!" Mary was very tired. But one kind man said, "I have a stable. It isn't fancy, but it is warm, and you are welcome to stay."

And that very night, in that quiet little stable, baby Jesus was born! Mary wrapped Him up snug in soft cloths and laid Him in a manger, which is a little box where animals eat their hay. It was Jesus's first bed. The cows and donkeys looked on, and everything was calm and still.

Now, on the hills outside Bethlehem, some shepherds were watching their sheep in the dark, quiet night. Suddenly... the sky lit up with a bright, shining light! The shepherds were so surprised! They covered their eyes and shook with fright. But the angel said, "Do not be afraid! I bring you good news that will bring great joy to everyone! Today, in the town of David, a Savior has been born! He is Christ the Lord. You will find Him wrapped in cloths, lying in a manger."

And then the whole sky filled up with angels! They sang, "Glory to God in the highest heaven, and peace on earth to the people He loves!" It was the most beautiful song the shepherds had ever heard!

When the angels went back to heaven, the shepherds said, "Let's go to Bethlehem and see what has happened!" So off they ran, as fast as their legs would carry them. And there they found Mary, and Joseph, and baby Jesus lying in the manger, just as the angel had said!

The shepherds were so happy! They told everyone what they had seen and heard. And Mary kept all these things in her heart, and thought about them quietly. The shepherds went back to their sheep, praising God for everything they had seen.

So Zara, why do we give presents at Christmas? Because God gave us the very first gift, His Son, Jesus! He came to love us. And now, let's sing "Jesus Is Born" together! Here we go!
