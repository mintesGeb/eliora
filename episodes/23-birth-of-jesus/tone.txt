A warm, expressive grandfather storyteller reading a picture book to small children. Friendly, bright, clear voice in a comfortable medium-high pitch, never deep or gruff. Natural, unhurried storytelling pace, like talking, not singing: no sing-song and no musical rhythm. Lively but natural character voices: a playful small voice for Eli, a cheerful voice for Zara, a gentle, kind voice for Mary and Joseph, a soft, hushed, wondering voice for the shepherds, a warm, bright and joyful voice for the angel, and a tender, quiet voice for the baby moments. Keep the same loudness and pitch from the first word to the last. Not shouting, not like a preacher, no exaggerated excitement, and no background music.
