Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch. Today Eli stood on his tiptoes to see the parade, but he was too small. Eli asked, "Grandpa, does Jesus see little people too?" So I said, come sit close, and I'll tell you about a short man in a tall tree.

One day Jesus came to a town called Jericho. Lots and lots of people came out to see Him. The streets were full and noisy!

In Jericho lived a man named Zacchaeus. He was a boss of the tax collectors, and he was very rich. But Zacchaeus took too much money from people, so they did not like him.

Zacchaeus wanted to see Jesus. But he was a short man, and the crowd was too big. He jumped up and down. Boing, boing! He still could not see.

So Zacchaeus had an idea. He ran ahead of the crowd. Pitter, patter, pitter, patter! He found a sycamore fig tree and climbed up, up, up into its branches.

Soon Jesus came walking down the road. He stopped right under the tree. Jesus looked up and called him by name. "Zacchaeus, come down at once! I must stay at your house today."

Zacchaeus hurried down the tree. Swish, thump! He was so happy. He took Jesus to his home and welcomed Him gladly.

But the people in the crowd began to grumble. Mutter, mutter, mutter! "Jesus is going to be the guest of a sinner," they said.

Then Zacchaeus stood up. He said, "Look, Lord! I will give half of everything I own to poor people. And if I cheated anybody, I will pay back four times as much."

Jesus smiled and said, "Today salvation has come to this house." Then He said, "The Son of Man came to look for the lost and to save them."

Jesus sees everyone and loves everyone, even when they have done wrong, and His love can change our hearts. You can say sorry, make things right, and share with others, just like Zacchaeus.

Now let's sing Come Down, Zacchaeus together. Climb up high with your hands, then jump down, with Eli and Pip! Ready? Let's go!
