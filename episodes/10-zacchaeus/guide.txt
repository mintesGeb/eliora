Summary: Short Zacchaeus climbs a tree to see Jesus, and Jesus calls him down, visits his home, and changes his heart so he gives to the poor and pays back what he took (Luke 19:1-10).
Memory line: Jesus sees me and loves me!
Question for ages 2-3: Where did Zacchaeus climb to see Jesus?
Question for ages 4-8: How did Zacchaeus change after Jesus came to his house? What can you do to make things right when you do wrong?
Prayer: Dear Jesus, thank You for seeing me and loving me. Help me share and make things right. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Make a "Zacchaeus tree": trace your hand and arm on paper as a tree, draw a little Zacchaeus in the branches, and write "Jesus sees me!" underneath.
