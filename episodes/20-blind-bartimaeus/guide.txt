Summary: A blind man named Bartimaeus calls out to Jesus even when the crowd tells him to be quiet. Jesus stops, calls him over, and heals his eyes (Mark 10:46-52).
Memory line: Jesus hears me when I call!
Question for ages 2-3: What did Bartimaeus shout? (Jesus, help me!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why do you think Bartimaeus kept shouting when people said be quiet? What did he see first? What would you like to tell Jesus today?
Prayer: Dear Jesus, thank You for always hearing me. Thank You for loving me when I call. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Close your eyes and listen quietly for one minute. Open your eyes and tell what you heard. Then say, "Jesus hears me!"
