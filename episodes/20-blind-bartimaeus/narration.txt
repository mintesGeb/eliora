Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were playing a game. Eli closed his eyes tight, and Zara said, "Can you find the door?" Eli shuffled, bumped the chair, and said, "Oof! It's so hard to see nothing!" Come sit close, Eli. I'll tell you about a man who couldn't see, and the day Jesus heard him.

Long ago, near a town called Jericho, a man named Bartimaeus sat beside the road. Bartimaeus could not see. He sat there every day, wrapped in his cloak, listening to the people walk by.

One day, Bartimaeus heard something different. Lots of feet. Lots of voices. Everyone was talking at once! "What's happening?" he asked. And someone said, "Jesus of Nazareth is coming!"

Bartimaeus's heart jumped! He had heard about Jesus. Jesus made sick people well! So he shouted as loud as he could, "Jesus! Son of David! Please help me!"

"Shh!" said the people. "Be quiet!" But Bartimaeus did not stop. He shouted even louder! "Jesus! Son of David! Please help me!"

And Jesus stopped. The whole crowd stopped! Jesus said, "Call him to Me." And the people who had said "Shh" now said, "Cheer up, Bartimaeus! Get up! Jesus is calling you!"

Whoosh! Bartimaeus threw off his cloak, and jumped to his feet! He felt his way toward Jesus, with a great big smile. Jesus asked him kindly, "What do you want Me to do for you?"

Bartimaeus said, "Teacher, I want to see." And Jesus said, "Go, your faith has made you well." And right then, Bartimaeus could see! The sky! The road! The faces of the people! And the very first face he saw was Jesus!

Bartimaeus was so happy, he leapt and danced! Then he followed Jesus along the road, thanking God with every step. And all the people who saw it praised God, too!

So Eli, what do we learn? When we call out to Jesus, He always hears us! He is never too busy to listen. And now, let's sing "Jesus Hears Me" together! Call out loud, friends! Here we go!
