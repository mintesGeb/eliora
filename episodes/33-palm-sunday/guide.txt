Summary: Jesus rides into Jerusalem on a donkey while the crowd cheers and waves palm branches (Matthew 21:1-11, Luke 19:28-40). Memory line: Hosanna! Jesus is our gentle King!
Question for ages 2-3: What did the people wave? (Palm branches!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did Jesus ride a donkey? What does Hosanna mean? How can we praise Jesus today?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for Jesus our gentle King. We praise You with our whole hearts. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Cut leaves from green paper, wave them and march around the room singing Hosanna.
