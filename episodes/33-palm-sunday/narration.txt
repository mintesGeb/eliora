Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were waving green leaves in the air and marching around the porch, and Pip the lamb hopped along behind them. "Grandpa Sol, we are having a parade!" said Eli. Oh, how wonderful! Come sit down, my dears, and I will tell you about the happiest parade in the Bible.

Long ago, Jesus and His friends were walking toward the big city of Jerusalem. Jesus had helped so many people. He had made sick people well, and He had taught everyone about God's love. Now Jesus was going to Jerusalem, and many people were waiting to see Him.

When they came close to the city, Jesus said to two of His friends, "Go into the village ahead of you. You will find a young donkey tied there that no one has ever ridden. Untie it and bring it to Me. If anyone asks, just say, the Lord needs it."

The two friends went, and they found the donkey, just as Jesus said. They untied it and brought it to Jesus. They put their cloaks on the donkey's back, and Jesus sat down on it. A king rode a donkey, to show He came in peace, not to fight.

Then the people saw Jesus coming! They were so happy! Some people spread their cloaks on the road, like a soft, warm carpet. Other people cut green branches from the palm trees and laid them on the path. Children waved their branches high in the air.

And everyone shouted, "Hosanna! Hosanna! Blessed is the One who comes in the name of the Lord!" Hosanna means, "Please save us!" The people knew that Jesus was God's own King, sent to save them. The whole road was full of singing and joy.

Jesus rode slowly through the happy crowd. He smiled at the children, and He waved to the people. He was not a king who wanted a big golden palace. He was a gentle king who came to love and to save.

Some people in the city were upset about all the cheering. But Jesus said, "Even the stones would cry out if the people stopped praising." Nothing could stop the praise that day!

So Zara, so Eli, what do we learn? Jesus is our gentle King, and we can praise Him with our whole hearts! Wave your leaves, my dears. Let's sing "Hosanna, Hosanna" together! Here we go!
