Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were carrying Pip the lamb in a blanket, like a little stretcher, and giggling. "Grandpa Sol, Pip is the one who needs help!" said Eli. Oh my, said Grandpa Sol with a smile, that reminds me of a story about four friends who carried someone they loved. Come sit close, my dears.

Long ago, Jesus was teaching in a house in a town called Capernaum. So many people came to hear Him that the house was full, and the doorway was full, and even outside was full. There was no room at all.

Now, in that town lived a man who could not walk. He lay on a mat all day, and he could not go anywhere by himself. But he had four good friends. They had heard that Jesus could help, and they said, "Let's take our friend to Jesus!"

The four friends picked up the mat, one on each corner, and carried their friend through the streets. But when they came to the house, they could not get in. The crowd was too big! Everyone was packed together like a basket of apples.

Did the friends give up? No! One of them said, "Look! We can go up on the roof!" In those days, roofs were flat, and people climbed up stairs outside the house. So the four friends carried the mat up, and up, and up to the roof.

Then they carefully made a hole in the roof, right over the place where Jesus was teaching. They lowered the mat slowly, down, down, down, on ropes. The people inside looked up in surprise, and bits of dust floated down!

Jesus looked up and saw the four friends and their great faith. He smiled. Then He looked at the man on the mat and said, "Friend, your sins are forgiven." And then Jesus said, "Get up, pick up your mat, and go home."

And do you know what happened? The man stood up! He picked up his mat and walked out, right in front of everybody! He was so happy, and he praised God. And the people were amazed and said, "We have never seen anything like this!"

So Zara, so Eli, what do we learn? Jesus is the best friend to bring our troubles to, and good friends help each other come to Jesus. Now let's sing "Bring Them to Jesus" together! Here we go!
