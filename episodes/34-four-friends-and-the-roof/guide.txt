Summary: Four friends carry a man who cannot walk to Jesus, lower him through the roof, and Jesus heals him (Mark 2:1-12, Luke 5:17-26). Memory line: Bring them to Jesus, He is kind and near!
Question for ages 2-3: How did the friends get the man to Jesus? (Through the roof!)
Question for ages 4-8: Why did the friends not give up? Who can you bring to Jesus in prayer? How did the man feel when he walked?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for good friends. Help us bring our friends and our troubles to Jesus. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Make a prayer list of friends and family to "bring to Jesus", and pray for each name together.
