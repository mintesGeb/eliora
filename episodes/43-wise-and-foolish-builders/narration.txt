Hello, friends! Welcome back to Grandpa Sol's porch! Today Zara and Eli were building a sandcastle in the sandbox, and Pip kept trying to hop right on top of it! Splash went the water bucket, and down came the castle. "Oh no!" said Eli. Oh, that reminds me of a story Jesus told about two builders. Come sit close, my dears.

One day, Jesus was teaching a big crowd on a green hillside. He wanted everyone to know how to live a happy, strong life. So He told them a story about two men who each built a house.

The first man was wise. He looked for a good place, and he dug down deep, and he built his house on a big, strong rock. Hammer, hammer, hammer! It was hard work, but he did it well.

The second man was in a hurry. He did not want to dig. He found some soft, sandy ground, and he built his house right there, on the sand. It looked pretty, and it was done very fast.

Then one day, the clouds turned dark. The rain came down, the rivers rose, and the wind blew and blew against both houses. Whoosh! Splash! Bang!

The house on the rock stood firm. The wind pushed and the water splashed, but it did not fall, because it was built on the rock. But the house on the sand? Oh my! The sand washed away, and down it fell with a great big crash.

Jesus smiled kindly. "Everyone who hears My words and does them," He said, "is like the wise man who built his house on the rock." My dears, Jesus is our strong rock. When we listen to Him and do what He says, our lives stand strong, even in the storms. Now Pip, are you ready? Let's sing about it! Here we go!
