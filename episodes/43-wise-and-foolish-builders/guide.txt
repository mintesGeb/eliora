Summary: Jesus teaches that listening to His words and doing them is like building a house on the rock (Matthew 7:24-27). Memory line: I build my life on Jesus!
Question for ages 2-3: Where did the wise man build his house? (On the rock!)
Question for ages 4-8: What happened to the house on the sand? Why did the house on the rock stand? How can we listen to Jesus and do what He says?
Prayer: Dear God, thank You for Jesus, our strong rock. Help us listen to Him and do what He says. Amen.
Activity (5 minutes): Build one small tower on a tray of sand and one on a flat board, then gently shake the table and see which one stands.
